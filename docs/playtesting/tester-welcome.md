# Starfall Protocol: welcome, tester

Thanks for helping test Starfall Protocol. It's a single-player sci-fi card game: you command a fleet across six sectors, building a deck as you go. It runs in your browser, on a phone or a computer.

**Play here:** https://blairot.github.io/starfall-protocol/

On a phone, add it to your home screen so it runs full screen and works offline. On iPhone, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. On Android, open the ⋮ menu and tap Install app.

## What to play

- Start a **New campaign** on **Officer** difficulty (the default).
- Play at your own pace. The most useful part for us is the start: the **Fleet Academy** (the tutorial) and the **Scrap Belt**, ending with the fight against Captain Vex. That's about two to three hours. Keep going if you're enjoying it.
- Sound on if you can. The cutscenes are voiced.
- Please don't look anything up or ask for help unless you've been stuck for a few minutes. Where you get stuck is exactly what we need to know.

## Telling us what you think

- After each battle, tap the **stars** to rate it, and add a note if something stood out.
- Any time, open **Settings → Save Data → Report a problem or idea**.
- When you finish a session (or a sector), go to **Settings → Save Data**, type your name, tap **Copy feedback code**, and paste the code into a message to me. On a phone, **Send…** opens your share menu instead.

Things we'd especially like to hear:

- Anything confusing, and the moment you first felt lost.
- Battles that felt unfair, too easy or too long.
- When you felt like stopping, and why.
- Your favourite and least favourite moments.

## What's recorded

The test build records your battles **on your device only**: which mission, win or loss, turns and minutes, the cards you played, your deck, your ratings and notes, and any script errors. The feedback code also includes your difficulty, campaign progress, screen size and browser type. Nothing leaves your device unless you copy the code and send it.

## Your save

Progress saves automatically in your browser. Clearing your browser data erases it. To keep a copy, or move to another device, use **Settings → Save Data → Copy backup code**, then **Restore from code** on the other device.

## Settings that might help

- **Display → Text size** makes story text, dialogue, tooltips, the combat feed and subtitles bigger.
- **Display → Shape markers** adds shapes to the green and red highlights. Turn it on if those colours are hard to tell apart.
- **Gameplay → Animation speed** speeds up the enemy's turn.
