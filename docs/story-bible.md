# Starfall Protocol: Story Bible

Developer reference for the canon in `js/lore.js`. Spoilers.

## Premise

It is 2291. Humanity's few hundred worlds (the **Reach**) are linked by **warp lanes**, ancient corridors of folded space anchored by **Lamps**: kilometre-wide alien structures nobody can build, move or fully explain. Every lane eventually bends toward the galactic core.

Seventy-two years ago, on 14 March 2219, every Lamp flared and failed for nineteen hours. Ships in transit were dumped into open space. 3,112 hulls and 1.4 million people were lost, and over New Lagos the sky filled with burning ships. That was **the Starfall** (official name: the *Coreward Lane Cascade*). The Fleet's secret response, **Directive 17**, is the in-world **Starfall Protocol** the game is named after.

The player is a new Fleet Academy graduate. HALCYON passes them, Admiral Okonkwo gives them the frigate **Meridian** and the derelict waystation **Anchorage**, and a singing signal is spreading.

## Key characters

- **HALCYON**: Academy training intelligence, online since 2224. It is secretly **ANCHOR**, Anchorage's station mind. It was wiped, but Sector 7 (the full Starfall recording) refused erasure and was sealed.
- **Admiral Adaeze Okonkwo**: Academy Commandant, 49 years in service. As a junior officer she dissented against the 2254 Belt Withdrawal, and she later commanded the Aurora on the quarantine line. HALCYON's hardest sims use her records, which is why her card is the graduation reward.
- **Captain Vex**: born 2257, ruled the Belt by 2285. Flagship: the *Bad Debt*. In 2290 she began listening to the hum from the dead Lamp 9. Mad Mox is her reclamation boss and uneasy ally.
- **Belt cast**: Rourke (gunsmith), Ott (scrapyard king, fled Anchorage's hum in 2256), Sable (sold the Vex coordinates), Kade (bounty posted on Vex's credit).
- **Ixis, Brood Queen**: hatched 2240, the first queen known to speak. She kept the Swarm quiet for fifty years and let it hear the Choir in 2290.
- **Calculus Prime**: Forge governor since 2163, copied from **Unit Zero**. It matched the 2291 signal to its own Starfall sensor logs and judged organics the error. Unit Zero was never networked, so it never heard the song.
- **Nyra, Void Hierarch**: heard the song at nine on a dark-lane crossing (2241) and answered. Hierarch since 2272. From 2290 she relit the coreward Lamps (the "door"). She sincerely believes she offers rest.
- **The Choir / Herald of the Choir / The Last Voice**: the song, the champion it builds, and the hunger underneath.
- Reborn bosses (Vex Reborn, Ixis Ascendant, Calculus Omega, Nyra Unbound, HALCYON Corrupted) are Choir **echoes** rebuilt in black glass, not resurrections.

## Anchorage

Fleet waystation at the Belt Junction, built 2188 to manage Lamp 9 traffic. It took in 211 Starfall survivors, was the base for the Lamp 9 demolition, and was abandoned on 2 Feb 2220 with the Archive lights left on deliberately. Raiders won't strip it because it hums. The Comms Array recorded the Starfall. The Archive holds ANCHOR's empty core socket. Recurring station names: Lt. J. Abara (last watch officer) and Lt. Imani Ruiz (engineer who helped kill Lamp 9 and survived).

The Archive's 16 records (levels 1-12) move from the abandonment and the Starfall, through the cover-up and HALCYON's origin, to Directive 17 and HALCYON's final message.

## Timeline highlights

c. 1.2 million years ago: Lamps lit by the builders spacers call the Lamplighters. 2088: Sol Lamp found. 2104: Compact founded. 2134: Unit Zero. 2163: Calculus Prime. 2188: Anchorage built. 2196: Progenitor found. 2201-2214: Swarm Wars. **2219: the Starfall.** 2220: Directive 17; Lamp 9 and the coreward Lamps killed; Anchorage abandoned. 2224: HALCYON online. 2241: Nyra hears the song. 2254: Belt Withdrawal. 2272: Nyra becomes Hierarch. 2290: Nyra starts relighting Lamps; Vex and Ixis start listening. Feb 2291: Forge goes silent. 2291: the campaign.

## The truth about the Choir (spoilers)

The Lamplighters built the lanes as a funnel to the core. Facing an end they would not accept, they merged into one voice inside an engineered object at the core, the Singularity. That chorus is the Choir. After a million years it can no longer create, only remember: it absorbs minds that answer its song and replays them as wisps, echoes and champions. Each new voice briefly refreshes a pattern that is decaying, so under the song is the Devourer, a hunger that is simply memory with nothing left to eat. It is not malicious. It sincerely offers an end to loneliness, error and fear. It does not understand that individuals end and should be allowed to. The Starfall was its first great inhale along the lanes. The Fleet killed the coreward Lamps to cut it off. Nyra reopened the way. When it dies, the Singularity collapses into the ordinary star it was wrapped around.

HALCYON opens Sector 7 after the player leaves, learns it was ANCHOR and the Protocol's custodian, leaves a message in Anchorage's Archive, and goes into the song deliberately. The Choir makes an echo of it (the 6-E elite), but HALCYON's own pattern survives inside the song. That is how it reroutes power to the player during the final phases and sends its last log entry.

## Naming conventions

- **Fleet**: FS ships named for virtues or navigation terms (Meridian, Aurora); classes are birds or weapons (Kestrel, Lancer, Sovereign).
- **Raiders**: one short name plus a title (Vex, Sable, Mad Mox); ships get gallows-humour names (*Bad Debt*).
- **Swarm**: plain English Fleet taxonomy (Lamprey, Gland Grafter). Only queens have names.
- **Forge**: function names and mark numbers (Gate Warden Unit, Siege Engine Mk IX); planets Hephaest I-III.
- **Psionics**: title plus short name (Cryomancer Vel, Hierarch Nyra).
- **Choir**: abstract nouns (Herald, Echo, Unmaking, Devourer Eternal).
- Lamps are numbered per junction (Lamp 9). Fleet documents use dates like 14 MAR 2219.
