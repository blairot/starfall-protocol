# Playtest report: Maya (card-game newcomer) · pilot-maya · build 60 · 2026-10-20

## Persona
Maya, 29, nurse. Plays match-3 and cosy sims on the bus in 10 to 15 minute bursts. Has heard of Hearthstone but never played a card game. Leaves difficulty on default (Officer). Plays by feel: deploys whatever she can afford, hits the enemy commander's face, rarely plans two turns ahead. Two losses in a row on one mission, or a loss she doesn't understand, makes her want to stop.

## Expectations (before playing)
"Space story with a sarcastic AI teacher. I think it's like Hearthstone but sci-fi? The first screen already says my deck is 13 out of 15 and has to be exactly 15, which is a bit scary before I've done anything. I hope it teaches me one thing at a time, and I hope I can finish a battle in one bus ride."

## Session summary (missions played, results, time-equivalent)
- Difficulty: Officer (default). Commander name: Maya.
- 1-1 First Contact: won T6, first try.
- 1-2 Fire Protocols: won T6, first try (launch was blocked at first, see incidents).
- 1-3 Hold the Line: won T8, first try.
- 1-4 Command Authority: won T8, first try.
- 1-E Sim: Iron Wall (Elite): skipped. Maya read "Most cadets do not get through this one" and "Starts with 8 Armor" (Armor never explained) and decided it wasn't for her.
- 1-5 Blitz Drill: won T7, first try.
- 1-6 HALCYON (boss): won T11, first try, finished on 6/30 Health.
- After graduation: watched cutscene s1, opened the Academy Pack (Dreadnought, Fleet Captain, Orbital Bombardment, Salvage Crew + a duplicate Ion Lancer), added all five new shiny cards to reach 20/20, read the codex, peeked at the 2-1 briefing.
- Record: 6 wins, 0 losses, no retries, so the Cadet retry offer never came up.
- Time-equivalent: about 50 to 60 minutes. Battles ran 6 to 11 turns, roughly 5 to 10 minutes each, so each one fits a bus ride. The boss is the longest.

## Critical incidents (chronological)

- [Start screen] CONFUSION. The first map screen says "Deck: 13/15 cards << the deck must have exactly 15 to launch". I hadn't played anything yet and already felt I'd done something wrong. 1-1 then said "This simulation uses a standard training deck", so the warning didn't matter yet. Severity: minor.
- [1-1 T1 to T2] CONFUSION. After I'd played my 1-cost card, the Advisor still said "Click a card in your hand that costs 1 to deploy it, then press End Turn". The tip doesn't update when you've done what it asked. Severity: polish.
- [1-1 T3, enemy turn] SURPRISE. My Ion Lancer (4/2) died the turn I played it, to a 2/1 Scout Drone. I learned that the second number is health, and that high attack doesn't mean tough. Severity: minor (fair, just a jolt).
- [1-1 Victory] OBSERVATION. The victory screen only says "New cards: Plasma Bolt, Rifle Squad, Recon Officer, Medic Bot. They're in your collection; add them to a deck in the Hangar." No line from Halcyon and no reaction to my win. It felt flat compared with the briefings. Severity: polish.
- [1-2 Briefing then Launch] CONFUSION / FRUSTRATION. HALCYON: "I have added Protocols to your deck: one-shot spells." I pressed launch and got "Your deck has 13/15 cards. It needs exactly 15." Plasma Bolt was not in my deck. I expected Halcyon to have added it, as she said. Instead I had to find my collection and pick 2 of 4 new cards with no guidance on which were good. I picked Plasma Bolt (because Halcyon talked about it) and Medic Bot (I'm a nurse). This is the first deck-building I've ever done, and it was forced on me by an error message. Severity: major.
- [1-2 T1, T2, T4] CONFUSION. Advisor tips named cards I didn't have: "Click Plasma Bolt, then click an enemy..." (not in my hand), "Rifle Squad asks for a target as it lands" (not in my deck because I didn't pick it), "Recon Officer draws a card" (not in my deck). I thought I was missing something. Severity: major (tutorial tips assume a deck the game didn't give me).
- [1-2 whole battle] OBSERVATION. I never drew Plasma Bolt in the mission meant to teach spells. I only learned what it does because the enemy used it twice on my units. I first cast it myself at the end of 1-3. Severity: minor.
- [1-3 T2] DELIGHT (small). I tried to hit the commander past a Guardian. The game said "a Guardian must be attacked first", which was clear. Halcyon's "Think of a Guardian as a locked door. Then think of something heavy." made it click. Severity: n/a (positive).
- [1-3 T3 to T6] BOREDOM / FRUSTRATION. The enemy commander stayed at 18/18 for five turns while it kept adding walls: Blast Barricade, Shield Trooper, another Barricade, another Shield Trooper, a Field Engineer buff, then a Bulwark Mech 4/6 Guardian. Every turn I chipped at walls and nothing felt like progress. Severity: minor.
- [1-3 T3 to T8] CONFUSION. The Advisor said "Your own Guardians protect you the same way. Use them to shield your fragile units." for six turns. I had no Guardians: the starter deck has none and I hadn't been told to add the Shield Trooper I'd just won. Severity: minor.
- [1-3 T7, 1-4 T6, 1-6 T8] FRUSTRATION. My Star Cruiser (6 Energy, my most expensive card) died on the enemy's next turn every time. Three small units ran into it one after another. My biggest card felt like a waste. I didn't understand why my big ship couldn't fight back on their turn. Severity: minor.
- [1-3 Victory] OBSERVATION. Two commander powers unlocked, and Nanite Repair was auto-equipped. I swapped to Pulse Laser because "Deal 1 damage" sounded like shooting. Picking was easy. Severity: n/a.
- [1-4 T3 onward] CONFUSION. The tip "At the start of a battle you can swap cards out of your opening hand. Send back expensive cards to find early plays." appeared on turn 3 and stayed until the end, long after the swap moment. It was good advice, but at the wrong time. I did use it at the start of 1-5. Severity: minor.
- [1-4 T8] DELIGHT. Vale was on 1 Health and I finished him with my 1-damage Pulse Laser. "Finished him with my laser!" Severity: n/a (positive).
- [1-4] OBSERVATION. The briefing says Vale has Pulse Laser. I never saw Vale use it in 8 turns. Severity: polish.
- [Map after 1-4] CONFUSION. The Elite says "Thick Hull: Starts with 8 Armor." Nothing has explained Armor, and it isn't in the codex. Together with "Most cadets do not get through this one", I skipped it. Severity: minor.

## Debrief answers
1. **How do you win, and three things I learned.** "You get the other commander's health to zero by hitting them with your ships. 1) You get one more energy each turn, so cheap cards early and big ones later. 2) Guardian units are walls: you have to knock them down before you can hit anything else. 3) Warp ships can attack the turn you play them, and Blitz ones can too, but only at ships, not the commander. Also, a ship's second number is how much it can take, and when two ships fight they both hit each other." She did not learn that units only protect you if they're Guardians, and she found out the hard way in the boss fight.
2. **Story so far.** "The space lanes are kept open by alien machines called Lamps. They all switched off seventy years ago and loads of people died. That's the Starfall. Now there's a signal from the middle of the galaxy. I'm a cadet being tested by HALCYON, a snarky training AI. I passed, Admiral Okonkwo gave me an old ship called the Meridian, and I'm going to the Scrap Belt because a dead Lamp has started humming. A raider called Captain Vex is out there too."
3. **Best, worst, most confusing.** Best: getting Orbital Bombardment and a Dreadnought from my first pack. In battle, coming back from 6 Health to beat HALCYON. Worst: the long wall-chipping in 1-3, and my Star Cruiser dying every single time. Most confusing: Halcyon saying "I have added Protocols to your deck" when she hadn't, then the game refusing to start.
4. **Difficulty curve.** "1-1, 1-2 and 1-4 were really easy. I just hit the face and won. 1-3 wasn't hard, just slow. 1-5 was the first real race. The boss was a big jump: I nearly died. So it's flat, flat, slow, flat, OK, spike."
5. **New cards.** "After every mission, which is nice, but the game never says 'put these in your deck'. It just says they're in my collection. Twice the lesson needed cards I had to add myself. The first pack was the best part. I can't afford another one yet."
6. **Cutscenes and story.** "They helped. Halcyon is funny and the bits are short. I read them all. The opening is clear. The briefings are short enough that I didn't skip them."
7. **Play again tomorrow?** "Yes, probably, to try my new big ships. What would stop me: losing twice in a row without knowing why, or having to manage all that Alloy and Anchorage stuff before I can just play."
8. **One sentence for a friend.** "It's a quick space card game with a sarcastic robot teacher, and it's easy to start, but read the cards because the AI fibs about what's in your deck."

## Top 5 findings for the designers (most important first, observation-based)
1. **The briefing says cards were added to the deck, but they weren't. At 1-2 the launch is blocked.** 1-2 HALCYON says "I have added Protocols to your deck". The deck stayed 13/15 without Plasma Bolt, and launch failed with "Your deck has 13/15 cards. It needs exactly 15." 1-5 has the same pattern for Warp units ("Today you learn to use them"). A newcomer's first deck-building comes from an error message. A player who just picks any two cards may skip the cards the lesson is about.
2. **Advisor tips name cards the player doesn't have and stay on screen after they stop applying.** 1-2 tips named Plasma Bolt, Rifle Squad and Recon Officer when none were in hand (two weren't in the deck). The 1-3 and 1-5 tips said to use your own Guardians when the deck had none. The 1-4 mulligan tip showed from turn 3 to the end. The 1-1 tip didn't change after the action was done. The boss tip says Target Practice hits "every turn", but it was used once in 11 turns, and that tip pushed me into a risky face race.
3. **The boss gave no opening-hand swap.** It was taught in 1-4 and offered in 1-4 and 1-5, but not in 1-6. I started with 5, 6, 2 and 1 cost cards and couldn't send the big ones back. If this is intended, the game should say so. If not, it's a bug.
4. **Difficulty is flat, then spikes. Officer was beaten 6/0 by "deploy what you can afford, hit the face".** 1-1, 1-2 and 1-4 were won on T6 to T8 with little danger (Health 23 or more). 1-3's challenge was slowness, not danger. The boss took me to 6/30. Repeated feel-bad: the most expensive card (Star Cruiser) died the turn after being played in 3 of 4 battles where I played it, swarmed by small units, with no hint why. I also wrongly believed units on the board protect your commander. Nothing corrected that until the boss nearly killed me.
5. **The graduation screen dumps a lot of new systems at once, and some words are never explained.** In one screen: Credits, Alloy, Scrap, Anchorage, contracts, a new pack set, deck size 20, a Legendary and a new power. Anchorage and Alloy aren't in the codex. Elsewhere the player sees words that are never explained: "Armor" (Elite 1-E), "tempo" and "Gear" (2-1 Tactics line). Positives worth keeping: HALCYON's voice, the clear Guardian refusal message, instant Warp attacks, and a first pack with an Epic and two Rares (the session's biggest high).

## Suggestions (optional, clearly labelled as opinion)
- (Opinion) When a mission teaches a mechanic, actually put 1 or 2 of that card in the deck, as the briefing says. Or show a "Add new cards to your deck?" prompt with the lesson's cards pre-selected.
- (Opinion) Only show Advisor tips about cards that are in hand, and clear a tip once its moment has passed.
- (Opinion) Give 1-3 or 1-5 a Shield Trooper in the deck. Add one line somewhere: "Units on the board don't block for you unless they have Guardian."
- (Opinion) Split the graduation rewards into two or three screens, and add Anchorage and Alloy to the codex.
- (Opinion) Make the first graduation Credits reward enough for one pack (125), or say "you can buy one after your next win."

## Harness issues (problems with the text interface, not the game)
- `help` before `new` errors with a stack trace ("No run "pilot-maya". Start one with: new"). `help` after `new` prints `Unknown command "help".` and then the usage text.
- Units renumber when one dies, so chained `attack` commands can hit the wrong slot. `attack A3 E1` after A3 had become empty printed the generic usage line instead of "no unit at A3".
- 1-5: "you go second", but no enemy turn 1 was printed before my turn 1. Energy showed "2/1" after Energy Cell. I can't tell whether that's the game or the harness.
- "You tear open the Academy Pack pack" repeats the word "pack" (could be the game's text).
- The victory output for 1-6 printed "No battle in progress. Use: launch <mission>" twice. This was because my follow-up attack commands ran after the battle ended.
- There's no harness command to look at Anchorage or spend Alloy, so I couldn't explore it. A player in the real game would at least see the screen.
