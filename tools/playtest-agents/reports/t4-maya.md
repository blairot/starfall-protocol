# Playtest report: Maya · t4-maya · build 68 · 2026-10-20

## Persona
Maya, 29, nurse. Plays match-3 and cosy sims on the bus in 10 to 15 minute bursts. Has heard of Hearthstone, never played a card game. Left difficulty on the default. Reads tips the first time, then skims. Plays by feel: deploys whatever she can afford and hits the commander's face. Two losses in a row on one mission makes her want to stop.

## Expectations (before playing)
"Space and a sarcastic robot teacher, I like that. The intro was short, which was nice. I'm guessing I put spaceships down and they shoot each other? I hope it explains things slowly and doesn't make me feel stupid. HALCYON saying 'most cadets fail' made me a bit nervous."

## Session summary
- New campaign on the default (Officer). Played 1-1 to 1-6 in order, skipped Elite 1-E (Maya wouldn't play an "optional" hard mission), then 2-1, 2-2 and 2-3.
- Results: 1-1 W, 1-2 W, 1-3 W, 1-4 W, 1-5 W, 1-6 **L** then W (retried on Officer; the Cadet retry was offered but Maya took one more go at normal first), 2-1 W, 2-2 W (ended at 6 Health), 2-3 W (ended at 11 Health; the enemy was on 1 Health).
- 10 battles, 9 wins and 1 loss. Battles ran 6 to 14 turns. About 5 to 12 real minutes each, so most fit one bus ride. 1-3 (14 turns, into fatigue) would not.
- Rewards: opened the Academy pack, bought and opened one Scrap Belt pack, added Admiral Okonkwo, used deck autofill, and picked Plasma Cutlass, Det-Bot and Shadow Corsair from crates.

## Critical incidents (chronological)

- **[Start screen] surprise · minor.** Status said "Deck: 13/15 cards << the deck must have exactly 15 to launch". On a brand-new game this read as "I've already done something wrong". 1-1 then said "This simulation uses a standard training deck" and launched fine. Expected: no deck warning before the first mission.
- **[1-1, T1] confusion · minor.** The first Advisor tip: "If a card in your hand costs 1, click it to deploy it." No card in my hand cost 1 (4, 3, 2, 2). The very first instruction didn't fit, so I just pressed End Turn and wasn't sure whether that was allowed.
- **[1-1, T2] confusion · polish.** Tip: "Units can attack the turn after they arrive. Click your glowing unit..." I had no units yet. Right advice, one turn too early.
- **[1-1, T3 to T5] frustration · major (teaching).** I hit the commander every turn as the brief said. The enemy kept attacking my units and trading them away, so by my next turn my board was always empty. The Advisor did say on T3 "Destroying enemy units keeps your commander safe", but I still won comfortably just by hitting face. **1-1 never made me fight units, and it didn't punish me for ignoring them.** I didn't learn that lesson until I lost 1-6.
- **[1-2, T1] confusion · polish.** Tip on turn 1: "Use spells on enemy units that your own units can't beat in a fair fight." I had no spells and there were no enemy units. "Fair fight" was also unclear to me.
- **[1-2, T4] delight.** I used Plasma Bolt on the 3/2 Rifle Squad because the tip told me to, and it died. The victory line "You spent your Protocols on the right targets" felt earned. (I don't know whether it says that no matter what you do. If so, a player who bolted face would find it odd.)
- **[1-2, reward] surprise · minor.** Every brief said "Reward: a supply crate of new cards", but in the Academy the cards were just loaded into my deck ("HALCYON has loaded them into your deck"). I never saw a crate until 2-1. Fine, but the brief over-promised.
- **[1-3, T2] confusion · minor (tip vs lesson).** The lesson was "destroy Guardians before you can attack anything behind them", but the T2 tip was "A Plasma Bolt can still hit the commander directly." The tip pushed me toward face again in the one mission about dealing with units.
- **[1-3, T3] confusion · polish.** Tip: "The Shield Trooper and Barricade HALCYON gave you are Guardians: use them to protect yourself." Neither was in my hand yet, so I didn't know what it meant.
- **[1-3, T3] honest mistake · minor.** I played Medic Bot and healed my commander at 30/30. The game let me "restore" Health I wasn't missing, and nothing told me it was wasted.
- **[1-3, whole battle] boredom · major.** 14 turns. Every turn the AI cleared my board with trades. Both decks ran out, and the last four turns were me poking Shield Troopers while fatigue ticked. It's too long for a bus ride and it's the dullest battle in the session. The fatigue rule was explained, but I only noticed it when "You ran out of cards: 1 fatigue damage" appeared.
- **[1-3 reward] surprise · polish.** It "swapped out Heavy Trooper, Patrol Frigate, Fleet Cadet": my two favourite big guys! It turned out to be duplicates only, and both were still in the deck. The wording scared me for a second.
- **[1-4, whole battle] confusion · minor.** I never used my commander power. Tips told me twice ("Check your power every turn. Spare Energy at the end of a turn is usually a power you forgot to use."), but I always spent everything on units, so the tip never fit my turn. Choosing a power in the Hangar was easy: "Deal 1 damage" sounded better than healing.
- **[1-5 brief] confusion · minor.** Warp ("Can attack the same turn it is deployed") and Blitz ("Can attack enemy units (not commanders) the turn it is deployed") were introduced together. I had to read them twice. I tested Blitz on the face and got "Can't attack that. Valid targets: E1". That message taught me better than the brief did.
- **[1-5] boredom · polish.** The "Strike Wing" was supposed to be fast, but it sat on a 5-card hand most of the game and I won on T7 without feeling any threat. "Learn to fear them" didn't happen.
- **[1-6 attempt 1, T8] unfair-feeling · major.** HALCYON had 5 units and I was on 10 Health. I went all-in on face and left HALCYON on 3, then died to four attacks. **Defeat screen:** "What hurt you most: Heavy Trooper 16 damage · Interceptor 6 damage · Card effects 3 damage". This was the most useful thing the game told me all session. Next try I killed his Heavy Troopers and won. But the Advisor line on the defeat screen was just the generic opener again ("This is the full exam: Guardians, Warp, Blitz and spells. Use everything you have learned."). It didn't say *why* I lost: that I was racing and ignoring his board.
- **[1-6 defeat] polish.** "That's 1 losses in a row here." (grammar). The Cadet offer after only one loss was reassuring. I didn't take it because I was close, but I'd have taken it after a second loss.
- **[1-6] observation · minor.** The T1 tip warned that "Target Practice deals 1 damage to your commander whenever it has Energy to spare", but in attempt 1 I never saw HALCYON use it, and in attempt 2 it used it once. The warning made it sound scarier than it was.

## Top 5 findings for the designers
1. **The Academy doesn't make you fight units.** Hitting face won 1-1 to 1-5 comfortably. The enemy traded my board away every turn without it costing me. The one tip about it ("Destroying enemy units keeps your commander safe") is outweighed by the brief's goal and by 1-3's "Bolt can still hit the commander" tip. I first learned it by losing 1-6 and reading "What hurt you most". (major)
2. **Advisor tips often don't fit the moment.** They're scripted by turn number, not by what's in my hand or on the board: a 1-cost card I didn't have (1-1 T1), attack instructions with no units (1-1 T2), a spell tip with no spells (1-2 T1), Guardians "HALCYON gave you" not yet drawn (1-3 T3), an Energy Cell when going first (2-1 T2). In contrast, the reactive tips (Boarding Crew, Mine Layer, Det-Bot) were excellent. (major)
3. **The defeat screen is half good.** "What hurt you most: Heavy Trooper 16 damage" was the single best teaching moment. But the Advisor line there just repeats the battle's opening tip and doesn't explain why I lost (I raced and ignored their board). Also: "1 losses". (major)
4. **1-3 Hold the Line drags.** 14 turns into mutual fatigue, a long string of poke-the-Guardian turns, too long for a phone session. 1-5 has the opposite problem: the "fast" enemy sat on its hand and posed no threat. (minor to major)
5. **New keywords arrive before they're explained, and the 2-2 turn order looks wrong.** Packs gave Salvage and Cloak cards before the briefs explained them, and `card` didn't define Salvage. In 2-2 the "you go second" mulligan was followed by me taking the first turn against an empty board (possible bug; 2-3's handling looked correct). (minor)

## Suggestions (opinion)
- Make one early Academy mission (1-1 or 1-2) unwinnable by pure face-racing. For example, an enemy unit that will kill you in 2 turns unless you deal with it, plus a tip that says so in that moment.
- Make the turn-based tips conditional on the hand and board (skip "play a 1-cost card" when there isn't one).
- On the defeat screen, add one sentence of cause: "Their units dealt 22 of your 30 damage. Try destroying big units before attacking the commander."
- Shorten 1-3 (smaller decks or lower enemy Health) so it ends before fatigue.
- Show a keyword's definition the first time a pack card has it.
- Warn when Medic Bot or Repair would heal a full-Health target.

## Harness issues (not the game)
- In Git Bash, an unquoted `#34` tag is treated as a shell comment. It had to be quoted (`'#34'`). The guide examples don't mention this.
- Hand numbers and `A#` slots shift after a draw (Recon Officer) or a deploy. Chaining `play 3` after a draw played the wrong card once (1-4 T5), and `attack A3` once picked a just-deployed unit (2-1 T7). Fixed tags help for units, but there are none for hand cards.
- `deck autofill` printed the deck listing twice.
- After the 1-6 victory, everything (rewards, systems unlock, cutscene prompt) arrives in one block. That's probably fine in the real UI.
