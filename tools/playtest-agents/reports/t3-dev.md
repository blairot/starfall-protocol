# Playtest report: Dev (the veteran) · t3-dev · build 65 · 2026-10-20

## Persona
Dev, 34, software engineer, Hearthstone Legend, years of Magic. Played on Admiral from the first mission. I skip the tutorial text, read card text closely, build decks on purpose, count lethal and look for exploits. I get bored without real decisions, and I get annoyed when I lose to a coin flip.

## Expectations (before playing)
The prologue reads like Hearthstone with a sci-fi campaign wrapped around it: Lamps, a signal from the core, a sarcastic training AI. I'm hoping Admiral really does mean "smarter enemies" and not just bigger health bars, and that deck building matters past the tutorial. I'm also expecting a 15-card tutorial deck to be dull for a while.

## Session summary
- **Sector 1 (Academy):** 1-1 to 1-6 and Elite 1-E. All won. 1-6 HALCYON finished with me on 7 HP. Everything else was comfortable.
- **Sector 2 (Scrap Belt):** 2-1 to 2-6 plus both Elites (2-E2 Salvage Rights, 2-E Bounty: Kade). Lost Captain Vex once, then rebuilt the deck and won. Four wins came with me on 7 HP or less.
- **Sector 3 (Hive Nebula):** 3-1 to 3-6 (Ixis), all won. 3-3 ended with me on 6 HP. I **skipped the two Sector 3 Elites (3-E2 and 3-E)** to make sure I reached Ixis. Dev would normally play them.
- Record: 21 wins, 1 loss. Roughly 6 to 7 hours of real play. Late Sector 3 battles ran 15 to 20 turns each.
- Final deck: 25 cards, built around Guardians and removal: Cluster Cannon, Pulse Laser power, toxic 1 and 2 drops, Okonkwo, Shipbreaker Titan, Vex, Railguns, Broadside and Mine Layers.

## Debrief answers
1. **How do you win?** Bring the enemy commander to 0. Units can't attack the turn they arrive unless they have Warp (any target) or Blitz (units only). Guardian forces attacks onto Guardians first; spells and area damage ignore it. Toxic destroys any unit it damages, including when it's defending. Your commander power costs 2 once per turn, and Gear lets your commander attack (taking damage back from units, but not from commanders).
2. **Story:** A cadet graduates under HALCYON and Admiral Okonkwo, is sent to the Scrap Belt to investigate Lamp 9's hum, and finds that Captain Vex was taken over by the Choir, a mind at the galactic core that offers people what they want. The Choir then turned the Swarm queen Ixis. The next lead is the silent Forge Worlds. Supporting characters: Mox, Sable, Kade.
3. **Best:** the Vex rematch, where a rebuilt deck beat a boss that had beaten me fairly. **Worst:** Det-Bot randomly killing Okonkwo in 2-5. **Most confusing:** the Apex Predator's promised enrage that never appeared.
4. **Difficulty curve:** the Academy is too easy on Admiral, apart from HALCYON. Sector 2 is the sweet spot, with real races and one honest loss. Sector 3 feels harder on paper but plays as long attrition. The Swarm AI is passive and heals, so it's grindy rather than dangerous.
5. **New cards:** a fair rate. Crates give a real 1-of-3 choice. Legendaries (Okonkwo, Vex, Ixis) feel strong. Cluster Cannon was the find of the run.
6. **Cutscenes/story:** I skipped most of it. The briefings are short and flavourful, and the Choir reveal lands. The text mismatches stood out more than the story did.
7. **Play again tomorrow?** Yes, for the Forge Worlds and to try other powers. The Sector 3 grind and the AI's self-destructive weapon attacks would stop me if they continue.
8. **To a friend:** "Solid Hearthstone-style campaign. Sector 2 on Admiral is genuinely good, but learn which bosses will suicide into your big units."

## Top 5 findings for the designers
1. **AI commanders with Gear attack big units and kill themselves.** Rourke went from 15 to 9 attacking a 6/3, Jun from 13 to 3 with two 1-attack pistol hits on a 5/5, and Kade lost about 20 HP to his own attacks. Any big unit invites it. This is the biggest exploit I found. The AI never weighs the damage its commander takes back against its own health.
2. **Admiral pressure is inconsistent, and the tactics labels don't match.** The AI did take exact lethal (Vex) and sometimes held a sweeper well (Vex's Broadside). But "Reckless" bosses (Tollmaster, Wing Leader, Vex retry) and the Swarm repeatedly passed on face damage with me at 6 or 7 HP. The Tollmaster never played Gear, and its hand sat at 5 for turns while Energy went unused (possible AI or card-playability bug, also seen with Ott).
3. **Handling big Guardians and units is the AI's strength.** It swarmed chaff into Okonkwo, used Shakedown and Life Drain on damaged units, played Mine Layer + Echo Protocol, avoided attacking my Mine Layer, and used toxic 1-drops on my Titan. Small misses: Railgun + Pulse not combined (1-5), and Nanite Repair cast with nothing to heal (1-E).
4. **Cluster Cannon and Pulse Laser look dominant.** Free face attacks plus a board sweep each time, three times, beat every swarm and Warp/Blitz deck. Pulse Laser had no rival among the powers. Toxic + Dig In is the cheapest answer to any big unit.
5. **Text and mechanics disagree in places.** Apex Predator promises an Alpha that gets stronger when wounded, and the victory text says it fell "in one blow" when I'd chipped it over five turns. Infest shows no effect in the log. Swarm decks run Fleet units. HALCYON auto-swaps your deck after Academy missions. The Advisor talked about the Energy Cell when I went first (2-1).

## Suggestions (opinion)
- Have the AI score a commander weapon attack by the damage it takes back against its own health. Never attack into a unit whose Attack is 25% or more of the commander's remaining health unless that attack is lethal.
- Make "Reckless" really go face when it is close to lethal, or relabel those bosses.
- Cap Cluster Cannon (for example, only when attacking a unit) or cost it 5.
- Make Admiral harder through decisions, not health pools: tighten Sector 1 sims, and make the Swarm bosses press for damage rather than trading forever.
- Show the Alpha's enrage, or rewrite the brief and victory text.

## Harness issues (not game problems)
- Unit and hand numbers renumber after every death or play. Chained commands often hit the wrong target. That cost me a lethal in 3-2 (Matron left on 1 HP) and caused several wasted actions. Failed attacks (for example, attacking past a Guardian) give no message in chained output.
- No command to spend Alloy (Anchorage) or Scrap (crafting), even though the game says both are open. 710 Alloy and 60 Scrap went unused.
- `open academy` fails; it needs the tier number (`open 1`).
- After `mulligan` the output only shows the new hand. I drew the same card back twice (Star Cruiser, Titan) with no indication of what was swapped.
- No mulligan step in 1-1 to 1-3 (possibly intended for the tutorial).
