# Playtest report: Dev (the veteran) · t5-dev · build 70 · 2026-10-21

## Persona
Dev, 34, software engineer. Hearthstone Legend, years of Magic. Plays on Admiral from the start, skips flavour, reads card text closely, builds deliberately, counts lethal. Gets bored when there's no real decision and annoyed by losses that come from variance rather than his own play.

## Expectations (before playing)
I was dropped in at Sector 4 with a save that had cleared Sectors 1 to 3: 94 cards, 760 Alloy and an autobuilt 30-card pile. I expected the late game on Admiral to make me rebuild around real synergies, and I wanted bosses with actual mechanics, not bigger health bars. My worry was that a single-player AI folds once you know its script.

## Session summary
- Played the whole late campaign in order: 4-1 to 4-6, 5-1 to 5-6, 6-1 to 6-5 (the finale). No optional Elites (I put the finale first).
- 25 battles in total: 17 wins and 8 losses. Two wins were Cadet retries (5-6 Nyra, and 6-5 the Choir), taken after Admiral losses so I could cover the finale. Everything else was won on Admiral.
- I rebuilt the deck about 8 times. Start: Pulse Laser plus a removal-heavy midrange list. Sector 5: added Psionic Null, more AoE and cloak units. Sector 6: legendaries plus Data Mine or Reinforce Plating.
- At about 10 to 20 turns a battle this is roughly 7 to 9 hours of real play. Forge fights dragged (20 turns in 4-2) and Sector 6 fights were dense.

## Critical incidents (chronological)
- **[4-1 to 4-6] boredom/major.** All six Forge enemies are "Methodical: ... rarely hits you until lethal is close." Several turns in a row the enemy played a unit and passed with no attack. Armor stacking (14 in 4-3, 17 on Calculus Prime) only made games longer. My HP after the Forge bosses was mostly 30 or more out of 36. The Admiral difficulty does not show up here. Severity: major.
- **[4-1 pack opening, then 4-4 briefing] confusion/minor.** I pulled Overclocked Mech, "Overheat (2)", from a Forge pack before 4-4. Nothing explained Overheat (the codex has no entry) until the 4-4 briefing. The same thing happened with Amplify, Cryo and Trap cards from Void packs. Also, "NEW: Twin-Fire" appears in the 4-3 briefing after I had already faced a Gatling Drone in 4-1. Severity: minor.
- **[4-2 T3] polish.** The briefing calls the mechanic "EMP: Wipes a unit". The combat log says "your Frenzied Ripper was silenced". "Silenced" is a different word for the same thing. Severity: polish.
- **[4-2 T17] AI quality.** EMP Trooper was spent on my Recon Officer, whose Deploy had already fired, so it was effectively vanilla. That wastes the card. Severity: minor.
- **[4-3 T6] delight.** A Nanobot with Barrier attacked my Toxic Hive Reaper: the Barrier absorbed the Toxic hit and the Reaper died. The AI knows Barrier beats Toxic. Nice.
- **[4-6] observation/major.** The 4-6 boss "Calculus Prime" has no phases and no unique rule beyond "Adaptive Armor". It played like 4-3 with more armor. The victory text says it "spends [its last seconds] talking", but I only saw the words in the s4 cutscene. Severity: major (boss feel).
- **[4-6 rewards] polish.** "Deck size increased to 30. Add 0 cards before your next battle." My deck was already 30. Severity: polish.
- **[5-1 onwards] delight.** Cunning AI uses Frost Lance and Mind Spike on my guardian, Dominates my best unit, and saves its burn for my face. The difficulty finally arrived.
- **[5-3, 3 attempts] unfair/major.** The Archivist's Probe Unit, Mind Seer and Telepath Scans kept its hand at 5 to 8 cards all game. ## Debrief answers
1. **How do you win?** Get the enemy commander to 0 while managing the board. Three mechanics the game taught well: **Barrier** blocks the first instance of damage, which makes it a hard counter to Toxic, and Calculus Prime's mass Barrier soaks AoE. **Overheat** gives cheap power now and locks Energy next turn. **Traps** are face-down triggers you bait with cheap plays (the Energy Cell is the cheapest bait of all).
2. **Story:** the Starfall killed a million people when the Lamps went dark. The Choir, the Lamplighters merged into one mind at the core, caused it. Calculus Prime and Nyra listened to it and relit the Lamps. HALCYON was the Anchorage station mind, and its secret order (Directive 17) was to train a commander to reach the core. Key characters: Okonkwo, HALCYON, Nyra, Vex/Mox, Unit Zero.
3. **Best:** 6-1 Vex Reborn, where Broadside plus Shakedown combos made me respect the AI. Also the Choir's phase transitions. **Worst:** the third Nyra loss. **Most confusing:** the 6-4 Advisor warning about two traps that didn't exist, and Foresight's "Scan" giving me no choice.
4. **Difficulty:** Sector 4 was flat and too easy (passive Methodical AI). Sector 5 was a cliff, peaking at Nyra. Sector 6 felt right on Admiral (6-1 to 6-4), then the finale's phase 1 spiked again. 5-6 is harder than 6-4, which is backwards.
5. **New cards:** crates every mission were good. Psionic Null, Void Oracle (Amplify +2 makes Acid Rain deal 3 twice), Calculus Prime and Lightning Arc all changed my deck. Packs felt meaningful. Overheat, Cryo and Amplify cards arriving before their rules were explained was the downside.
6. **Cutscenes:** I skim them, but s5 ("What the Choir Is") and the finale earn their place. The HALCYON payoff ("Evaluation complete. Commander, you pass.") landed.
7. **Play again tomorrow?** For Sector 6 on Admiral, yes. To grind Nyra again, no.
8. **One sentence:** A tight, fair-feeling single-player CCG with a genuinely smart late-game AI, let down by a sleepy Forge sector and one Sector 5 boss that's tuned wrong.

## Top 5 findings for the designers
1. **Difficulty curve is inverted in the late game.** Forge (Sector 4) on Admiral is too easy: passive Methodical AI, no boss phases, I usually ended above 30 HP. The 5-6 Nyra boss then beat me 3 times on Admiral with three different archetypes (my best left her at 26/36), while her echo 6-4 Nyra Unbound fell in 11 turns. The finale's phase 1 (21 HP) took me from 40 to 5 by turn 8.
2. **Scan has no faction limits, so enemies get unbounded card advantage.** The Archivist's hand sat at 5 to 8 all game, and Scan brought in off-faction cards: The Hatchery (Cloak, refills Swarmlings) plus Infest made boards that AoE can't clear. Losses felt random rather than earned.
3. **Hidden information leaks.** The combat log names the enemy's armed Trap ("played Sniper Mine"), and the Advisor names Nyra's starting Psionic Null and spoils every Choir phase. One Advisor tip (6-4, "Two Traps are armed") was simply wrong.
4. **A 0-cost Energy Cell triggers Psionic Null.** That gives a free trap bait for both sides. The AI wasted my Null on its own Cell, and I used the same trick to clear Nyra's Null at no cost.
5. **The AI is excellent in Relentless and Cunning mode.** Examples: Broadside ping into Shakedown; EMP/Disruptor on its own units to clear Freeze or Overheat; Disruptor on my Titan to strip Salvage before trading; Barrier attackers into Toxic. Methodical mode just passes turns. Give Forge enemies some of that aggression.

## Suggestions (opinion)
- Give 4-6 Calculus Prime a phase or a unique rule, and let Methodical enemies attack when they're ahead on board.
- Restrict Scan to the caster's own faction and set pool, or cap enemy hand size lower.
- Reduce 5-6 Nyra's Mind Blast or take one Dominate out of her deck, or swap her difficulty with 6-4.
- Make the Energy Cell not count as a "spell" for Traps and for Psionic Horror or Nanoforge triggers.
- Explain Overheat, Cryo, Amplify and Trap in the Codex the moment the player first owns such a card.
- Don't name the enemy's armed Traps in the log, and keep the Advisor from spoiling boss phases.

## Harness issues (not the game)
- Hand indices renumber after every card played, so chaining `play 3` then `play 3` easily hits the wrong card. This cost me Energy Cells twice (5-2, 5-3) and directly lost Choir attempt #2 (Railgun cast with no target instead of Lamprey). A play-by-name option would fix it.
- `play` with a target-required spell and no target fails quietly in chained output. Easy to miss.
- Foresight, Probe Unit and Mind Seer "Scan" gave me no `choose` prompt; the card was auto-picked.
- Piping output through `tail -0` crashes `play.js` with EPIPE (my own fault, but there's no graceful handling).
- The log says "silenced" while the game term is EMP. That may be harness wording.
- The `card` command needs card ids (`assembler`), not display names. Quoting the name works.
- Alloy and Anchorage can't be spent anywhere in the harness.
