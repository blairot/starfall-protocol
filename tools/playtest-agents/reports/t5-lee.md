# Playtest report: Lee · t5-lee · build 70 · 2026-10-21

## Persona
Lee, 52, back to games after years away. Slower to learn rules and makes honest mistakes: plays the biggest card he can afford, goes for the commander's face, forgets which slot is which and misses plays. Starts on Officer and drops to Cadet when the game suggests it or after repeated losses. Mostly keeps the auto-built deck; adds a new legendary because it's shiny.

## Expectations (before playing)
"I've apparently already cleared three sectors, so I know roughly how it goes: put units down, knock out the other commander. I hope the game keeps telling me what the new tricks are, because I won't work them out alone. If I get stuck, I hope it tells me what to do differently."

## Session summary
- Started at Sector 4 with the typical save (`--from 4`), on Officer, with the auto-built 30-card deck. Only changes before the late game: Plasma Bolt out, Calculus Prime in (after 4-6). Bought and opened 1 Forge Pack and 1 Void Pack.
- **Sector 4 (Forge Worlds):** 4-1 to 4-6 all cleared on Officer. 1 loss (4-5), won on retry. The boss (4-6) went down first try.
- **Sector 5 (Void Expanse):** 5-1 to 5-4 won on Officer. 5-5: lost once, then won. **5-6 Nyra:** lost on Officer, lost the offered Cadet retry, switched the whole game to Cadet, lost again. **That was Lee's quit point.** Then (past the quit point) I let `deck autofill` rebuild the deck and won on the 4th attempt.
- **Sector 6 (Singularity, all on Cadet):** 6-1 and 6-2 won. **6-3 Calculus Omega: lost twice. I conceded the second attempt at turn 13.** Its health had not dropped since turn 4: 21 health plus 14 armor, and I had one card in hand. **Lee did not reach 6-4 or the final boss 6-5.**
- Totals: 21 battles, 14 wins, 7 losses. About 4 to 5 hours of real play at Lee's pace. Many battles ran 12 to 20 turns.

## Debrief answers
1. **How do you win?** Get the other commander to 0. Three things I learned: (a) a **Guardian** has to be attacked first, and EMP can strip its Guardian; (b) **Armor** soaks damage before Health, and some bosses gain it every turn; (c) **Amplify** units make the enemy's spells hit harder, so kill them first. Also: Toxic kills anything it damages, and Barrier blocks one hit.
2. **Story:** The Choir (the Lamplighters, now one mind at the galactic core) caused the Starfall. Calculus Prime and Nyra listened to it, and Nyra relit the Lamps. HALCYON was secretly the Anchorage station mind told to train me, and it has gone into the Choir. Okonkwo and Mox guide me. Now I'm fighting Choir "echoes" of old bosses on the way to the core.
3. **Best:** beating Calculus Prime first try, and the Sector 5 cutscene. **Worst:** losing Nyra on Cadet and getting the same advice. **Most confusing:** Minefield, where her health didn't move for 16 turns (Kinetic Barrier) and my legendary got stolen.
4. **Difficulty:** Sector 4 was mostly fair, with one grindy armour slog (4-2). 5-1 to 5-3 were fine. The spike starts at 5-4 (traps) and gets brutal at 5-5 and 5-6 (spell burn + Dominate + freeze). Sector 6 on Cadet was easy for 6-1 and 6-2, then a hard wall at 6-3.
5. **New cards:** The crates and packs were frequent enough, but I mostly didn't use them; I don't know which ones matter. The legendary I added got frozen, stolen or copied in every Void fight.
6. **Cutscenes:** They added a lot. Short, with clear reveals. The Sector 4 and 5 endings are the best part of the session.
7. **Tomorrow?** Honestly, no. I'd be stuck on Calculus Omega with no idea what to change. Maybe I'd come back if the game told me *what to put in my deck*.
8. **To a friend:** "Good story and easy to pick up, but the last two worlds will beat you up if you're not a card-game person."

## Top 5 findings for the designers
1. **A struggling player can't finish the late game: Lee hit a hard wall at 6-3 Calculus Omega, even on Cadet, and was only just carried through 5-6 Nyra.** The cause in both cases was running out of cards. The typical deck has one Supply Drop as its only card draw. Late enemies win the long game with armour gain, board refills, steal effects and traps. Against Omega, its effective health didn't change from turn 4 to turn 13 because my chip damage only matched its armour gain.
2. **`deck autofill` actively makes this worse.** It removed the only card-draw card and ignores the 4-5 briefing's own advice ("Bring cards that draw cards"). The defeat screen's only deck advice is "Adjust your deck in the Hangar". For Lee, "the game builds my deck" is the main way to adjust, and it gave him a deck with no card draw.
3. **The defeat-screen advice is good against one dominant unit and useless against spell or trap damage.** The good ones: "Storm Titan 36 damage" and "Tri-Walker 40 damage" led straight to wins. But all three Void losses got the same line ("Card effects N damage … Keep your Health higher, and finish the fight before they add up"), which doesn't say to kill the Amplify units or to bait traps. It also contradicts itself across retries ("finish the fight" vs "destroy their units before racing"), and it credits damage from a Mirror Field copy of my own card to "Calculus Prime". Nothing mentions card draw, deck changes or the new Data Mine power.
4. **The Cadet safety net is uneven.** It's offered only after boss losses: nothing at 4-5 or 5-5. It's offered again right after a Cadet loss ("You may retry this one battle on Cadet"). On Cadet, Nyra's health drops 28 → 18, but her burn, freeze, Mirror Field and Dominate felt unchanged. Omega on Cadet still gains armour every turn. Cadet stops being a fallback in Sector 6.
5. **Rules and log bugs in the Void and Singularity sectors make losses feel unfair:**
   - a Dominated unit attacked on the turn it was stolen (contradicting the rule text);
   - "? took N damage / ? was destroyed" lines;
   - the enemy's starting trap vanishing with no log line (twice);
   - "Dominate on **enemy** X" for my own unit;
   - Kinetic Barrier, Mirror Field and Barrier pops not explained in the log;
   - "Can't attack that" without saying it's because of Cloak;
   - an orphan "was transformed" line;
   - "Add 0 cards" text;
   - the Advisor naming Pulse Laser when it isn't equipped, and saying a trap is armed after it had fired.

## Suggestions (opinion)
- After 2 losses on a mission, have the Advisor or defeat screen name one concrete deck change ("Add Supply Drop / Data Mine: you ran out of cards on turn 9"). Make autofill keep at least 2 or 3 card-draw cards.
- Make the "Card effects" advice spell-specific: name the spell or Amplify unit, and say "bait traps with a cheap unit first".
- Offer Cadet after 2 losses on any mission. On Cadet, also tone down boss mechanics like Adaptive Armor gain and burn damage, not just Health.
- Log trap effects in plain words ("Kinetic Barrier: Ilu gains 8 Armor").
