# Playtest report: Lee (the struggling player) · t3-lee · build 65 · 2026-10-20

## Persona
Lee, 52, coming back to games after years away. Slow to learn rules, makes honest mistakes: attacks the commander when units should be traded, plays the biggest affordable card, forgets Toxic, mis-trades into big units. Started on Officer. Planned to drop to Cadet if the game suggested it or after repeated losses.

## Expectations (before playing)
"A space card game with a sarcastic computer teacher. The intro says most cadets fail, which worries me a bit. I hope it explains things one at a time and doesn't punish me for being slow. If I lose, I want to know what I did wrong."

## Session summary
- Played 1-1 to 1-6, 2-1 to 2-6 and 3-1 to 3-6 in story order. Skipped all Elites (1-E, 2-E, 2-E2) because "optional" and "Elite" sounded like trouble to Lee.
- Results: 18 wins, 3 losses. Sectors 1 and 2: no losses. Sector 3: lost 3-1 once (won the retry on Officer), lost 3-6 Ixis twice on Officer, then took the offered Cadet retry and won.
- Roughly 3.5 to 4 hours of real play time. Battles were long: most went 10 to 13 turns.
- Commander power: Nanite Repair (heal 2) the whole way. Lee never switched.
- Deck building: Lee used `deck autofill` when the deck size went up, and made a few swaps of the "this new card looks good" kind (Heavy Rail Rifle, Broadside, Cluster Cannon, Acid Rain, Life Drain in; Supply Drop, Ion Lancer, Combat Stims, Twin Pistols out).

- **[After each Academy win] delight.** "HALCYON has loaded them into your deck for the next lesson, swapping out…" For Lee, not having to build a deck early was a relief. Severity: n/a (positive).

## Debrief answers
1. **How do you win?** Get the other commander's Health to zero. Units hit back when you attack them, so pick fights where you survive. Guardians have to be killed before you can hit anything behind them, but spells skip them. Toxic kills any unit it scratches, so kill those with spells or your commander's weapon. If you run out of cards you start taking damage.
2. **Story so far?** The Lamps that run the space lanes went dark once. Now a signal from the galaxy's centre, the Choir, promises people what they want and then takes them over. It took Captain Vex in the Scrap Belt and Ixis the Swarm queen. Next is the Forge machine worlds. Characters: HALCYON (sarcastic training AI), Admiral Okonkwo, Mad Mox, Sable, Vex, Ixis.
3. **Best:** Cluster Cannon wiping four Swarmlings. **Worst:** watching Ixis heal from 24 to 32 when I attacked her guard. **Most confusing:** an enemy Echo Protocol killing my Field Engineer, and fatigue appearing in 1-3 before anyone explained it.
4. **Difficulty:** Gentle and long through Sector 1, with lots of deck-out stalemates. Sector 2 was manageable because Okonkwo and Vex carried me. Sector 3 was uneven: 3-4 was the easiest battle of the run, and 3-6 was a wall on Officer.
5. **New cards:** Frequent and exciting (crate after every mission, legendaries from bosses). Packs had a lot of duplicates ("duplicate beyond the limit: +5 Scrap", and I didn't know what the limit was until I read the codex). Area-damage cards were the meaningful ones.
6. **Cutscenes/story:** Short and readable. Vex's log ("I said yes.") landed. They never got in the way.
7. **Play again tomorrow?** Probably, because the Cadet offer got me past Ixis and I want to see the Forge. I'd stop if the next boss is another healing wall with no hint.
8. **To a friend:** "A friendly space card game that teaches you well, until a boss suddenly gets very hard."

## Top 5 findings for the designers
1. **Ixis on Officer is a hard spike for a struggling player, and the Cadet offer arrives one loss late.** 34 to 0 in five turns on attempt 1. On attempt 2, Lamprey Sentinel Siphon healed Ixis 8+ when I attacked into it (40 health pinned, then 24 to 32). Lee hit her quit point there. The Cadet retry (Ixis 26 health) was fair and won. The offer only appears after 2 losses in a row.
2. **Briefings and Advisor tips often describe things that don't happen in that battle.** Salvage (2-2), Cloak (2-3), the Lamprey "wall" (3-5), the Devourer Worm and the Alpha's "angrier when wounded" (3-4), Boarding Crew before it existed (2-1). Ixis's tip says she summons "two Swarmlings every turn", but her power text says one. Also, each battle shows one tip repeated every turn instead of reacting to the board.
3. **Siphon and other "your attack triggers their effect" interactions aren't clear.** I didn't realise attacking a Siphon Guardian heals the enemy commander. The same kind of confusion hit with an enemy Echo Protocol killing my unit and a Det-Bot's random Salvage killing my best unit. The log shows the result, but nothing tells Lee why.
4. **The Academy's 15-card decks run into fatigue, and fatigue isn't taught until after it happens.** It first hit me in 1-3 and was explained in 1-4. 1-3 and 1-4 went to T12, and the HALCYON final exam ended with HALCYON dying to its own fatigue while I pressed heal on 4 health. Long and anticlimactic for the graduation fight.
5. **Swarm battles hinge on whether you draw area damage, and rewards (legendaries) carry weak play.** Lee won with Broadside, Acid Rain or Cluster Cannon in hand and lost without them (3-1 first try, 3-6). Okonkwo and Vex won most of Sectors 2 and 3, so Lee never had to learn trading well before the Ixis wall. (Also: in 3-2 and 3-4 the AI left full boards idle and never attacked.)

Positives worth keeping: the Academy's auto-loaded decks, the turn-1 Advisor instructions, the clear error messages ("a Guardian must be attacked first", "can't attack now… unless they have Warp or Blitz"), "What hurt you most" on the defeat screen, and the Cadet retry itself.

## Suggestions (opinion)
- Offer "Retry on Cadet" after the first boss loss, or after any loss where the player's commander died before enemy Health dropped below ~75%.
- Make the defeat screen name one concrete action, e.g. "Swarm bosses: put Broadside / Acid Rain in your deck" or "Attacking Siphon units heals their commander. Use spells on them."
- Teach Fatigue in 1-3's briefing (or give Academy decks 20 cards), so HALCYON can't die to fatigue in the final exam.
- Only show "NEW: X" in a briefing when that mission's enemy actually uses X.
- Add "Siphon (including when it is attacked)" or similar to the Siphon definition, if that's the intended rule.

## Harness issues (not the game)
- **Index shifting.** After a kill or a play, E-numbers and hand numbers renumber. Several times my next command hit the wrong target or played the wrong card ("Can't play Dig In: No valid targets", a Shield Trooper played instead of a Medic Bot, a Field Engineer attacking the Devourer Worm instead of a larva). A click UI wouldn't have this problem.
- **Unnamed units in the log.** During Ixis's Acid Rain the log showed "? took 1 damage." / "? was destroyed." (probably my Junk Drone).
- **Odd log line.** The larva transform prints as "    enemy Brood Warrior was transformed." with extra indent and no "Hive Larva" mention.
- **No Anchorage command.** The help lists none, so 560 Alloy went unspent and I couldn't judge the Anchorage loop.
- **Victory output.** If you pipe the final attack through `head`, the victory/reward text gets cut off (my own mistake; `status` recovered it).
