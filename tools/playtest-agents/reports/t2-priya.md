# Playtest report: Priya (the story player) · t2-priya · build 63 · 2026-10-20

## Persona
Priya, 41, teacher. Mass Effect, The Expanse, narrative games. I play for the characters and the world; the battles are how I get through the story. Officer difficulty, average skill: I play sensibly but don't optimise. I read every briefing, victory screen and cutscene. I care about voice, consistency, motivation and whether the story *lands*.

## Expectations (before playing)
The prologue sold me on it: seventy-year-old disaster (the Starfall), alien "Lamps" that hold the warp lanes open, a signal from the galactic core, machine worlds going quiet, and a sardonic academy AI. I expected The Expanse with a card game bolted on: a cadet who graduates, gets a ship and walks into a mystery. I hoped every mission would move that mystery forward a little and that the characters would *react* to what I did.

## Session summary
- Sector 1 (Fleet Academy): 1-1 to 1-6 played in order, all won first try. Skipped the Elite 1-E "Sim: Iron Wall" (no story, just a stress test, so Priya wouldn't bother).
- Graduation cutscene s1 watched.
- Sector 2 (The Scrap Belt): 2-1, 2-2, 2-3, Elite 2-E2 "Salvage Rights" (played because it's about *my* station, Anchorage), 2-4, 2-5, Elite 2-E "Bounty: Kade" (played because it's a bounty on *me*), boss 2-6 Captain Vex. All won first try.
- Sector 2 cutscene s2 watched.
- One out-of-persona replay of 2-1 at the end, to try to recover a victory text I'd lost (see Harness issues). It showed no story text, only the replay reward.
- Result: 15 wins, 0 losses (14 campaign + 1 replay). Roughly 2 to 2.5 hours of real play.
- Deck management was light: took HALCYON's prebuilt decks in Sector 1, added Admiral Okonkwo and Fleet Captain at graduation and autofilled the rest. Later swapped Supply Drop for the Shipbreaker Titan (story reward, "back rent"), and added Vex's legendary plus an autofill before Sector 3. Opened every pack. Never spent Alloy (no Anchorage screen in the harness).

## Critical incidents (chronological)

- **[Prologue]** delight. The opening is tight and specific: "Seventy years ago, every Lamp went dark at once... We call it the Starfall." Then HALCYON: "Most cadets fail. I mention this purely for context." I knew the world, the stakes and a character's voice in under a minute. Severity: n/a (positive).
- **[Star map after `new`]** surprise. The status line says "Deck: 13/15 cards << the deck must have exactly 15 to launch", but the 1-1 briefing then says "This simulation uses a standard training deck." For a second I thought I had to build a deck before I'd played a card. Severity: polish.
- **[1-1 victory]** boredom. The victory screen is only "New cards: Plasma Bolt, Rifle Squad, Recon Officer, Medic Bot. HALCYON has loaded them into your deck for the next lesson." No HALCYON quip. The same happens after 1-2, 1-3, 1-4 and 1-5. The briefings have so much HALCYON personality that the silent victories feel like the character walked out of the room. Severity: minor.
- **[1-4 briefing]** delight then disappointment. "Vale is based on several of your instructors. I will not say which. They know." Great line. But Vale has no dialogue in the battle or afterwards, so the hook goes nowhere. Severity: minor.
- **[1-5, after win]** boredom. Five missions in, the story hasn't left the simulator. The prologue promised a galactic signal and a Swarm, and I've had five lessons. The pacing is fine for teaching rules, but for me the Academy ran one mission too long before anything *real* happened. Severity: minor.
- **[1-6 briefing]** surprise. Admiral Okonkwo's first mention is "Admiral Okonkwo is watching from the gallery." She's about to become the second most important character, and this is the first time I hear her name. Severity: polish.
- **[1-6 victory text]** confusion (lore). "Lamp 9, shut down by the Fleet seventy years ago, has started humming." The prologue says "Seventy years ago, every Lamp went dark at once" and calls that the Starfall. So did the Fleet shut the Lamps down? Is the Starfall the Fleet's fault? If that's a deliberate secret, nobody in the story reacts to it. The s1 cutscene then calls it "a dead Lamp", which blurs it again. Severity: major for a lore reader (either a contradiction or a buried twist).
- **[Cutscene s1 Graduation]** delight. HALCYON: "You're the first this year to pass my final exam without crying. I'm counting the shouting separately." Okonkwo: "Giving you Anchorage was my idea." HALCYON: "Her idea, from my request log." That exchange makes both of them people. The distress call ("Our machines stop working when it gets loud") ties back to the prologue's "machine worlds have gone quiet". HALCYON's "I wasn't built to believe in coincidence" links the hum to the galactic signal. This cutscene earns its time. Severity: n/a (positive).
- **[Cutscene s1 vs 1-6 victory text]** boredom. The victory text has already told me about the commission, the Meridian and Lamp 9's hum. The cutscene then tells me all three again. Severity: polish.
- **[HALCYON's voice, across Sector 1]** observation. In briefings HALCYON never uses contractions ("I have added Protocols", "I will not say which", "I will be your opponent"). In the cutscene it suddenly does ("You're the first", "I'm counting", "I wasn't built"). The formal voice is part of its charm, so the shift reads as a different writer. Severity: polish.
- **[Graduation rewards]** surprise. "Deck size increased to 20. Add 5 cards before your next battle." It was easy enough with autofill. My commander's max Health also went from 30 to 32 in Sector 2 and to 34 later. I never spent Alloy, and nothing told me why. Severity: minor.
- **[2-1 briefing]** delight. Okonkwo: "The Fleet does not pay tolls to raiders, Commander. Go through him." Okonkwo as the voice on the comms in Sector 2 is a great choice. Captain Vex is seeded by name ("Captain Vex gets her cut either way"). Severity: n/a (positive).
- **[2-1, turn 7-8]** delight. Playing the Admiral Okonkwo card and having it deal the final blow felt like a story moment. Severity: n/a.
- **[2-1 battle]** surprise (lore polish). The raider tollmaster fields "Fleet Cadet" and "Colonial Marine" units, and so do Rourke and Ott. Raiders running Fleet cadets breaks the fiction a little. Severity: polish.

None. The closest was after 1-5: five simulations with silent victory screens, and I was starting to skim. The 1-6 victory text and graduation cutscene pulled me back in. ## Debrief answers

1. **How do you win?** Bring the enemy commander's Health to zero. Three mechanics:
   - **Guardian**: units with it must be attacked first. It's also the only thing that "blocks"; my other units don't protect me.
   - **Warp vs Blitz**: Warp units can attack anything, including the commander, the turn they arrive. Blitz units can only hit enemy units on that first turn.
   - **Salvage**: a unit's effect fires when it dies (Scrap Golem leaves two Junk Drones). Killing it isn't always free, and Det-Bot's random 2 damage kept hitting my face.
   Also learned: Gear arms your commander with Attack/Durability, attacking with it hurts you too, and Cloak hides a unit until it attacks.
2. **What's the story so far?** Seventy years after every Lamp went dark (the Starfall), a signal from the galactic core is waking things up. I graduated from HALCYON's academy, Admiral Okonkwo gave me the frigate Meridian and the ruined station Anchorage, and I went to the Scrap Belt because dead Lamp 9 started humming. Raider queen Captain Vex had let a voice from the Lamp, the Choir, into her ships in exchange for the Belt. With help from her disillusioned salvage boss Mox and the smuggler Sable, I broke through her people and her flagship, the Bad Debt. The Choir took Vex over and she died. Her log says the Choir wants the Swarm in the Hive Nebula next. Main characters: HALCYON (dry academy AI), Admiral Okonkwo (protective, blunt), Mox (pragmatic ex-raider), Captain Vex (tragic villain), Sable (smuggler). Vale, Rourke, Ott, Jun and Kade are names attached to fights.
3. **Best moment?** The s2 captain's log: "The voice calls itself the Choir. It says the Belt can be mine. I said yes." Runner-up: exact lethal on Ott at 9 Health. **Worst moment?** Beating Sable and getting no word from her, then finding the payoff a mission later in someone else's briefing. **Most confusing?** Mox speaking in the 2-E2 Elite briefing before I'd met him, and "Lamp 9, shut down by the Fleet" against the prologue's "every Lamp went dark at once."
4. **Difficulty over the session:** Sector 1 was easy and steady. Sector 2 rose to its peak at 2-3 (Ott's junk swarm) and the Jun Elite, where I was nearly dead. Then it *dropped*: 2-5 and the Vex boss were easier than 2-3. The boss was the easiest fight in Sector 2. That's backwards for a climax. Dull stretch: 1-2 to 1-5, story-wise.
5. **New cards:** Frequent enough. Graduation was the high point: an Admiral Okonkwo legendary is a story reward that means something. Crates mostly offered cards for keywords I hadn't learned yet (Gear, Salvage, Cloak), so I picked blind. The Shipbreaker Titan as "back rent" and Vex as a legendary are the right idea: rewards tied to the story.
6. **Did the cutscenes and story help or get in the way?** They're the reason I kept going. Both cutscenes earned their time. s1 for the HALCYON/Okonkwo banter and for wiring the distress call to the prologue; s2 for the Choir reveal and Vex's log. The briefings are consistently good, short and in character. The problem is what happens *between* briefings: most wins have no story beat, so the plot advances in a stop-start way and the villains never get a last word.
7. **Play again tomorrow?** Yes. I want to know what the Choir is, whether the Fleet really shut the Lamps down, and what happens with the Swarm. What would stop me: more stretches of silent victories, or a Sector 3 boss that folds as easily as Vex did.
8. **One sentence to a friend:** "It's a pleasant card game wrapped around a genuinely intriguing space mystery with a very funny AI, but the story only talks to you before the fights, never after them."

## Top 5 findings for the designers (most important first)

1. **Most wins have no story beat.** Of 13 first-time wins, only the 1-6 boss and the 2-E2 Elite (Jun) showed victory narrative. The other 11 showed only rewards. Each villain gets a great opening line in the briefing (Rourke, Ott through Mox, Sable, Kade's setup, the Wing Leader) and then nothing when beaten. Story consequences arrive in the *next* briefing instead: Sable's coordinates are only confirmed in the 2-5 briefing. The 2-E2 victory ("Call it back rent.") shows how much one or two lines add. (major)
2. **Mox is introduced three times, the first time before he exists.** Elite 2-E2 unlocks after 2-1 and its briefing quotes "Mox:" with authority. 2-3 then introduces him ("Meridian, this is Mox. I strip wrecks for Vex."). The s2 cutscene introduces him *again* ("Meridian, it's Mox. I ran salvage for Vex.") under a new name, "Mad Mox", never used before. (major)
3. **The boss climax under-delivers in play and in story.** Vex never used her advertised Cannon Volley in 7 turns and died to one 13-damage turn. She was the easiest fight in Sector 2 (2-3 and the Jun Elite were far harder). Then the cutscene kills her off-screen in one line ("Then the channel went quiet. Captain Vex was dead.") with no cause given. Related AI passivity: in 2-E2, Jun had lethal on board against my 6 Health with no units for several turns and didn't attack; in the 2-1 replay the raiders stopped attacking once I had a Guardian out. Villains that don't press their advantage drain the tension the writing builds. (major)
4. **Lamp lore reads as a contradiction.** The prologue says every Lamp went dark at once seventy years ago (the Starfall). The 1-6 victory says Lamp 9 was "shut down by the Fleet seventy years ago"; s1 calls it simply "a dead Lamp"; Vex says "The Fleet left the Belt to rot." If the Fleet caused or hid something about the Starfall, that's a fantastic twist, but right now nobody notices or reacts, so it reads as an error. Either have a character flag it (HALCYON or Okonkwo would) or align the wording. (major for lore readers)
5. **What's working, so protect it.** HALCYON's voice is the best thing in the game ("I am told this is where I say congratulations. Congratulations."), with one wobble: no contractions in briefings, contractions in cutscenes. The Vex arc lands emotionally in very few words ("Nobody ever promised me anything before."; "I said yes."). Okonkwo as the comms voice in Sector 2 gives continuity. The s1 and s2 cutscenes earn their time. Smaller polish items in the same area: the Vex legendary card arrives seconds after her death with no framing, raider decks field "Fleet Cadet" and "Colonial Marine" units, and "Ghost Signal" promises a signal that isn't in the mission.

Also important but outside story: every Gear card displays as "N/undefined gear" with no Durability (e.g. "Shock Baton [1 Energy, common gear 2/undefined gear]"), while the same gear shows a proper "2/1" once equipped in battle. This could be the game or the harness; please check.

## Suggestions (opinion, clearly labelled)
- Give every story mission a 1 to 3 line victory beat: the beaten villain's parting shot plus Mox/Okonkwo/HALCYON reacting. A line for Vale ("Tell Instructor ___ I said hello") would make 1-4 sing.
- Gate the 2-E2 Elite behind 2-3, or rewrite its briefing so Okonkwo or HALCYON speaks instead of Mox.
- In s2, let Mox acknowledge he's been on comms all along ("You know me by now...") and either earn "Mad Mox" earlier or drop it. Say *how* Vex died: the Choir burning her out is stronger than silence.
- Make the Vex boss use Cannon Volley reliably, and give her a mid-fight line when she drops below half Health.
- Have HALCYON or Okonkwo react to "shut down by the Fleet" in the s1 cutscene, even with a deflection ("That file is above your clearance, Commander.").
- Frame the Vex legendary card (e.g. "Vex's old crew, signing on").
- Make Advisor tips react to the cards actually on the board.

## Harness issues (text interface, not the game)
- **My own error, flagged so it isn't mistaken for a game bug:** I piped command output through `head` and `>/dev/null` during attacks and twice cut off the victory screen: 2-1 (first clear) and 2-6 (the boss's first-clear victory text). So I can't say whether 2-1 or 2-6 had narrative victory text. Replaying 2-1 showed only "+15 Credits (replay reward)". The s2 cutscene covered the 2-6 beat.
- `open academy` returned "No unopened pack of that tier." It needs the tier number (`open 1`). Minor.
- `codex` printed nothing at all before graduation. After graduation it printed the full Archive. That might be intended, but an empty print looks like an error.
- Pack opening reads "You tear open the Academy Pack pack:" (word doubled), probably harness string formatting.
- "N/undefined gear" on Gear cards in `card`, crate and pack listings. It could be harness formatting of a Durability field (in battle, gear shows correctly, e.g. "Plasma Cutlass 3/1").
- Card flavour text renders with an extra leading quote when a speaker is named (`"Vex: "...`), possibly harness wrapping.
- No harness command for Anchorage, so 420 Alloy went unspent and I couldn't see what raised my commander's Health from 30 to 32 to 34.
- `card shipbreaker_titan` says "No such card." (the id is `shipbreaker`; the display name works). Fine, just noting.
- `deck autofill` prints the full deck listing twice.
- Unit indices (E1, A2...) shift as units die mid-turn, so a chained command sometimes hit a different unit than I meant. Expected for a text harness; no game impact.
