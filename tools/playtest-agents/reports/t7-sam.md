# Playtest report: Sam (the collector) · t7-sam · build 74+ (contracts fix) · 2026-10-21

## Persona
Sam, 23, student, gacha/collectible player. Officer difficulty, decent skill. Opens packs right away, checks the collection all the time, plays every Elite, chases progress bars. This was an ECONOMY session, so I engaged with every currency and system between battles: packs, crates, Anchorage, crafting, scrapping, signature card and contracts.

## Expectations (before playing)
"A save file with 410 Alloy, 50 Credits and an Anchorage with 8 modules? Yes please. I expect a base-building meta layer where my station grows alongside my collection, and I hope every battle pays something I can spend straight away: a pack, a module level or a craft."

## Debrief answers
1. **How do you win?** Get the enemy commander to 0 Health; armour goes first. Three mechanics I learned: **Toxic** destroys any unit it damages, so cheap Toxic units trade up into anything. **Barrier** eats the first hit, so pop it with Pulse Laser before hitting for real. **Overheat** locks that many Energy next turn, so use it when you can afford a slow next turn, or when the enemy has just overheated.
2. **Story?** The Starfall killed millions. A Choir signal from the galactic core is corrupting leaders (Ixis the Swarm queen, then Calculus Prime of the Forge), and Calculus's logs say the signal *caused* the Starfall. Okonkwo is my admiral, HALCYON the snarky AI, and Unit Zero is waking up.
3. **Best / worst / most confusing?** Best: the 3-E2 payout screen, and Progenitor making three Hivewardens. Worst: the boss crate in 4-6 offering three cards I'd maxed. Most confusing: what Archive and Observatory actually do.
4. **Difficulty?** Sector 3 was mostly easy with the Toxic deck. Sector 4 Elites and 4-5 were a clear step up, driven by armour walls and EMP stripping my keyword units. That wasn't a spike so much as a length increase.
5. **New cards?** Frequent and exciting for the first few packs of each set. Hive went stale fast (heavy rare duplicates). Forge stayed fresh longer, then repeated Deflector Grid and Chain Lightning. New legendaries came from Elites and Bosses, and those were the highlights.
6. **Cutscenes?** I skimmed, as Sam would. The Calculus "signal caused the Starfall" reveal was a cool hook.
7. **Play again tomorrow?** Yes: I'm 1 short on a Raider contract, Medbay Mk 3 is 120 Alloy away, and Sector 5 packs should be fresh. What would stop me is if Sector 5 packs dry up like Hive did.
8. **One sentence for a friend:** "Packs and contracts are genuinely moreish, but half your base upgrades don't seem to do anything yet."

## Top 5 findings for the designers
1. **Three Anchorage modules showed no observable effect, and they took 47% of my Alloy (520 of 1,110).**
   - **Trade Dock Mk 1 to 3:** mission Credits always equalled the briefing amount. After Mk 3 ("Supply Packs cost 90") the Depot still listed 125 and charged 125 (bought at 245, left 120).
   - **Observatory Mk 1 and 2:** briefings never showed key cards or a deck list.
   - **Archive Mk 1 and 2:** nothing to view anywhere.

   Med Bay, Fabricator, Comms and Armory (signature) all visibly worked. The Dock price is a hard bug; the Observatory and Archive may be harness rendering, so please check.
2. **Duplicate-heavy rewards undercut the collection loop.**
   - Acid Rain turned up 7 times and Deflector Grid 8 times.
   - Elite rewards named cards I had already maxed: the Acid Rain at 3-E2, and the Deflector Grid at 4-E2, which was quietly turned into Scrap.
   - The 4-5 and 4-6 Boss crates offered only cards at the 2-copy limit.
   - The last 3 Hive packs had 1 new card in 15.

   Scrap softens this (~690 Scrap earned), but the moment-to-moment feeling is "nothing new".
3. **Contracts are the real economy, and they work well.** They paid 1,050 Credits (45%) against 720 from missions. They progressed sensibly after every battle and changed how I played: Arm Blade for "attack with your commander", swapping Synth cards in, the Elite goal. Triple-completion spikes (+181, +233) were highlights. Two issues: token summons silently don't count as "Deploy" (fair, but unstated), and faction contracts like "Play 15 Raider cards" can stall for many battles if your deck changes. Comms Mk 2 (+50%) was the best-value module.
4. **Crafting is affordable only at the low end, and hard to aim.**
   - Over 17 battles I could afford 2 commons, 1 rare and 1 epic.
   - A legendary (1,280 even at Fabricator Mk 2) was out of reach.
   - Nothing in the collection shows which cards I'm missing (it only says "99 of 175"), and the total silently grew from 152 to 175, so I couldn't target crafts.
   - I crafted a card and then pulled the same card from the next pack.
5. **Pacing and clarity at the Sector 3 to 4 boundary.**
   - The opening Alloy dump (410 Alloy, Mk 1 at 30 each) is a superb first impression.
   - After that, Mk 2 at 70 and Reactor Mk 3 at 170 against ~30 Alloy per mission means one upgrade every 2 to 3 battles.
   - Forge packs unlock right after 3-6 and hand out Overheat and EMP cards before the game teaches either: `card` and `codex` have no definition, and the briefing explains Overheat only at 4-4.
   - Story missions 3-4 and 3-5 have no crate, which is a noticeable reward dip.

## Suggestions (opinion)
- Make Trade Dock's bonus visible on the victory screen ("+30 Credits, +9 Trade Dock"), and fix the Mk 3 pack price.
- Give Archive a viewable "Records" tab or unlock list, and show Observatory info in the briefing.
- Bad-luck protection for rares, or no repeat rare within one pack. Crates and Elite rewards should prefer cards below the copy limit, or say "(+20 Scrap, you have the max)".
- Collection view with silhouettes of missing cards per set, so crafting can be aimed. Don't move the "of N" total without a "New set: Forge (23 cards)" notice.
- Add Overheat and EMP to the codex and card text the moment the first such card is obtained.
- State "from your hand" on Deploy contracts, and consider a contract reroll.

## Harness issues (text interface, not the game)
- `craft` with no argument **crafted Fleet Cadet** (−30 Scrap), and `scrap` with no argument **scrapped Fleet Cadet** with no confirmation. I lost a card and 30 Scrap while trying to see the usage text.
- `deck remove lamprey_sentinel` / `deck add lamprey_sentinel` → "No such card", but the name "Lamprey Sentinel" worked. Tri-Walker's id is `war_walker`. Ids aren't always guessable.
- No commands for Armory deck slots (Mk 1 says "keep up to 3 decks"), so I couldn't test that feature.
- `collection <filter>` only matches names: no rarity, faction or missing filter.
- Battle log omissions:
  - When an attacker and a defender both die to Toxic, the enemy's "was destroyed" line is sometimes missing.
  - Barrier pops and armour absorption aren't logged. Attacks into armour print no damage line.
  - When a Ghost Operative was played without a target, its Deploy ping simply didn't happen, with no prompt.
- The opening hand said "you go second", then showed my turn 1 before any visible enemy action. Turn order in the log is hard to follow.
- When I chained commands, a victory occurring mid-chain made later commands report "No battle in progress". That was my fault with `head`, but a clear "battle over" message on each later command would help.
