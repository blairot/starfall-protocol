# Playtest report: Priya (the story player) · t4-priya · build 68 · 2026-10-20

## Persona
Priya, 41, teacher. Loves Mass Effect, The Expanse and narrative games, and plays for the characters and the world. Officer difficulty, average skill: plays sensibly but doesn't optimise. Reads every briefing, every victory text and every cutscene closely. This run checks whether the revised story works: victory lines for every mission, Mox's introduction, Lamp 9's history, Vex's death and the Ghost Signal mission.

## Expectations (before playing)
The prologue is tight and makes a strong promise. There are four hundred worlds and alien Lamps keep the lanes open. The Starfall killed a million people, there's a signal from the core, and the Swarm is breeding again. Then HALCYON's dry "Most cadets fail. I mention this purely for context" got a smile. I expect an Expanse-flavoured mystery about the Lamps and the signal, told through a card game. I hope the characters are people and not just quest-givers.

## Session summary
- **Played:** Sector 1 (1-1 to 1-6), Sector 2 (2-1 to 2-6, plus Elites 2-E2 Salvage Rights and 2-E Bounty: Kade) and Sector 3 (3-1 to 3-6). I skipped Elites 1-E, 3-E2 and 3-E, but read their briefings.
- **Results:** 21 wins and 2 losses.
  - **Losses:** 3-1 Venom Fields on the first try, and 3-6 Ixis on Officer.
  - **Retries:** won 3-1 on a second Officer attempt after rebuilding the deck. Won 3-6 on the offered Cadet retry.
  - **Replay:** replayed 2-1 once (see harness issues).
- **Cutscenes watched:** Prologue, s1 (Graduation), s2 (The Bad Debt) and s3 (The Brood Queen).
- **Time-equivalent:** about 3.5 to 4 hours.

## Critical incidents (chronological)
- **[Start screen] confusion.** The first status screen says "Deck: 13/15 cards << the deck must have exactly 15 to launch", but 1-1 then uses "a standard training deck". For a few seconds I thought I had to build a deck before I'd played a card. **Severity: minor.**
- **[1-1 to 1-6] delight.** HALCYON's victory lines are consistently funny and in voice. Each one points at the next lesson, so the Academy feels like a course and not a list of fights. **Severity: (positive).**
- **[1-6 victory text and cutscene s1] boredom and repetition.** The victory text already covers a lot:
  - Okonkwo signs my commission and gives me the Meridian.
  - She orders me to the Scrap Belt because Lamp 9 has started humming.
  - It explains Lamp 9's history.

  The s1 cutscene then repeats nearly all of it. Okonkwo also re-introduces herself ("Commander, I'm Admiral Okonkwo") even though the victory text had her sign my commission a moment earlier. Two related details:
  - The text says "Lamp 9" while the cutscene says "Lamp Nine". It's a tiny inconsistency, but I notice that sort of thing.
  - Lamp 9's history ("When the Lamps came back on after the Starfall, the Fleet shut Lamp 9 down for good as a safety measure") is the first time I learn the Lamps came back on at all. The prologue only says they went dark. It's also the only place this history appears, buried in a victory paragraph and not in the cutscene, where it would land.

  **Severity: minor.**
- **[1-6 brief] polish.** The reward line spoils the cameo: "Admiral Okonkwo (Legendary)". **Severity: polish.**
- **[2-1] surprise and difficulty spike.** The first Belt mission took me from 32 to 7 Health. I replayed it once (to recover victory text I'd missed) and dropped to 7 again. After six gentle Academy fights it's a jump. Priya would cope, but a newer player might not. **Severity: minor.**
- **[2-E2 vs 2-3] Mox introduced twice.** The optional Elite 2-E2 appears at the same time as 2-3 and has its own Mox introduction: "Mad Mox, who runs the salvage crews for Captain Vex and has never liked Jun". 2-3 then introduces him again, properly: "Mox here. I strip wrecks for Vex. Or I did, until she started talking to that Lamp…". The 2-3 version is good and gives the whole sector its motive (stop Vex before the Lamp gets everyone killed). Either order works, but if you play the Elite first, Mox gets introduced twice. **Severity: polish.**
- **[2-4 brief] confusion: a bounty from nowhere.** In the Ghost Signal briefing Mox says Sable "also brokered the bounty on your head, so don't feel bad about it." Playing in story order, I had never heard of a bounty. It's explained only in the optional Elite 2-E Bounty: Kade, which unlocks at the same time and which I hadn't played yet. A story mission depends on optional content. **Severity: minor.**
- **[2-4 Ghost Signal] delight.** This mission works:
  - The briefing explains the ghost signal in one clear sentence.
  - Sable has a voice ("I want to see if you're worth betting on").
  - Her Cloak units *are* the "ships run dark" line.
  - The victory pays it off: the coordinates "arrive on a ghost signal nobody could trace", and Mox's "nicest thing Sable has ever said to anyone" made me laugh.

  The revision landed. **Severity: (positive).**
- **[2-E victory] polish.** Mox: "Forty-one for forty-one, then you." I had to reread it to get "Kade was 41 for 41 until you." **Severity: polish.**
- **[2-6 brief] delight.** Vex: "The Fleet left the Belt to rot before I was born… Nobody ever promised me anything before." That's a villain with a reason. **Severity: (positive).**
- **[2-6 victory text and cutscene s2] Vex's death.** It now reads clearly in the cutscene: "As the Bad Debt lost power, the Choir took her mind with it… Captain Vex was dead." Her log's "I said yes." is chilling. Three problems remain:
  1. **Over-explained.** The victory text summarises the Choir: it offers what you want, takes people over, promised Vex the Belt, took her and is singing to the Swarm. The cutscene then says the same things three more times: in Vex's transmission, in her log and in Mox's summary. By the fourth telling, the reveal had lost its punch.
  2. **Cause of death.** The victory line "it took her" is vague. In the cutscene it's unclear whether I killed her, the Choir did, or the power loss did. I'd like to know whether to feel guilty.
  3. **Her card.** Right after her death the game hands me "Vex, Scourge of the Belt (Legendary)" to put in my deck. Playing a dead woman as a unit felt odd, with no line explaining it (a salvaged ship? her crew joining me?).

  **Severity: minor (story feel).**
- **[2-6 cutscene] polish.** Vex's log says "New course: the Hive Nebula", but she was parked at Lamp 9 when I found her. Did she ever leave? **Severity: polish.**
- **[2-6 victory → Sector 3] frustration with the deck size jump.** After Vex the deck grows to 25. I used autofill (that's what Priya does), and the autofilled deck had almost no area damage going into a Swarm sector. I only learned that "area damage hurts it most" after losing. The 3-1 briefing teaches Toxic but doesn't warn me to bring area damage. (The Tactics line and an Advisor tip say so only once the battle starts.) **Severity: minor.**
- **[3-1, first attempt] unfair and confused.** Here's what happened:
  - Hive Larva "transformed" at the start of the enemy's turn and attacked straight away for 3. Damage I'd put on the larva was wiped by the transformation.
  - Toxic units ate my big units.
  - I lost from 34 Health.

  The defeat screen was good: "What hurt you most: Hive Reaper 12 damage…". Some of the loss was my own misplays, made through the text interface (see harness issues). **Severity: minor.**

Each briefing is a mechanic lesson dressed up (Siphon, the breeding chamber, the Alpha, the royal guard). The Choir only comes back in the 3-6 boss briefing. Sector 2 kept me hooked because every briefing moved the Vex hunt along (Mox → Ott → Sable → the Wing Leader → Vex). Sector 3 reads like a nature documentary until the last mission. One line per briefing would fix it ("the nebula pulses in time with the Choir's song"). **Severity: major (for a story player).**
- **[3-3 victory → 3-4] confusion.** "Our scouts say something big is hunting them further in." I read "them" as the Swarm (something hunting the Swarm? exciting!). In 3-4 it's a Swarm hunter that picks off *our* scouts. **Severity: polish.**
- **[3-4 victory] mismatch between text and play.** The briefing said "Kill it in one blow… If you only hurt it, you just make it angry." The victory text then says "The Alpha goes down in one blow, which was the only way it was going to go down." I wore it down over about four turns, and nothing visible made it "angry". The text contradicts what I just did. **Severity: minor.**
- **[3-E briefing] lore.** "older than the Swarm Wars" is the first and only mention of any Swarm Wars. Is that the quarantine? When? I wanted one more clause. **Severity: polish.**
- **[3-5] boredom.** Queen's Guard became a 14-turn grind. Swarmlings kept respawning, and Siphon and Life Drain healed the enemy back up. I was worn down rather than tense. **Severity: minor.**
- **[3-6 on Officer] frustration and difficulty spike.** Ixis has 40 Health and healed back to 40 twice (Life Drain, Lamprey Siphon). Hive Surge and Spawn Surge put seven Swarmlings on the board, and I lost from 24 Health in two turns. After one boss loss the game offered the Cadet retry right away. As a story player I was grateful and took it. That's the right design: the Cadet version (26 Health) was a fun 12-turn fight. **Severity: minor (the safety net works).**
- **[3-6 briefing, victory text and cutscene s3] delight, plus some repetition.**
  - **Ixis's briefing is strong:** "Who are you to tell them to stop?" Okonkwo's "She kept the peace for fifty years… Now she is leading an army. End it." gives the kill moral weight.
  - **The cutscene:**
    - HALCYON returning ("They were very loud. I didn't complain.") is a joy.
    - The Forge reveal ties back to the prologue's "machine worlds have gone quiet". Real payoff.
  - **Problems:**
    - **A repeated template.** Ixis's confession copies Vex's almost exactly: "It promised… so I said yes. After that, it spoke through me." If it's deliberate, it's a good motif, but two bosses in a row as sympathetic victims who explain themselves at the end now feels like a template.
    - **The Progenitor arrives out of nowhere** as a card-art aside.
    - **"since February"** jars in a 2291 setting. It reads like a real-world date and it's oddly precise.
    - **Her card.** I get "Ixis, Brood Queen (Legendary)" right after "With Ixis dead". Same dissonance as Vex.

  **Severity: minor.**

## Quit points
None. The closest was after losing 3-6 on Officer. Priya would have closed the app if there were no Cadet retry. The offer kept her playing. Earlier, Sector 3's plot-less middle (3-2 to 3-5) was where her attention drifted. She kept going because she expected the Choir to come back, and it did, but late.

## Debrief answers
1. **How do you win, and three mechanics I learned.**
   - **How to win:** bring the enemy commander to 0 Health by deploying units, trading them with enemy units and attacking the commander.
   - **Guardian:** enemies must attack Guardians first. That also blocks *my* units and gear, which I forgot twice.
   - **Toxic:** destroys any unit it damages, so I answered it with spells, small units or my own Toxic Venom Tender.
   - **Siphon:** damage it deals heals its commander, even when it's the one being hit, which is why Ixis kept healing.
2. **The story so far, and the main characters.** A mind at the galactic core, the Choir, uses humming Lamps to promise people (and creatures) what they want most, then takes them over.
   - **Captain Vex** said yes to rule the Belt, and it killed her.
   - **Ixis**, the Swarm queen, said yes to free her children, and died too.
   - **The trail:** now it leads to the Forge, the machine worlds that build our ships.
   - **The cast:**
     - **HALCYON** is the dry Academy AI.
     - **Admiral Okonkwo** is my steady, wry mentor.
     - **Mad Mox** is a defector from Vex's crews.
     - **Sable** is the smuggler queen.
     - **Rourke and Kade** are memorable one-scene rogues.
3. **Best, worst and most confusing moments.**
   - **Best:** Vex's briefing line ("Nobody ever promised me anything before") and the Ghost Signal mission.
   - **Worst:** losing to Ixis on Officer while she healed to full twice.
   - **Most confusing:** Mox mentioning "the bounty on your head" before any story mission had told me about one.
4. **How difficulty changed.** The Academy was gentle (2 out of 5). Sector 2 jumped straight away at 2-1, and I finished several Belt fights at 7 or 8 Health. Sector 3 spiked at 3-1 (my first loss) and again at the boss. 3-5 was a dull grind.
5. **Getting new cards.** Getting cards often felt nice, and the Legendaries (Okonkwo, Vex, Ixis) are story mementoes I wanted to use. On the other hand, the deck growing to 25 and then 30 pushed me to autofill, and autofill doesn't prepare me for the next sector.
6. **Did the cutscenes and story help or get in the way?** They mostly add, and they're why I kept playing. But the victory text often summarises the cutscene that plays right after it, so each big reveal is told two or three times.
7. **Would I play again tomorrow?** Yes, to see the Forge and find out whether Calculus Prime said yes too. I'd stop if Sector 4 is another four plot-less filler missions before a boss.
8. **One sentence for a friend.** "It's a card game with a surprisingly human space-opera story, especially the Scrap Belt, and an AI tutor who's funnier than she should be."

## Top 5 findings for the designers (most important first)
1. **Sector 3's middle missions (3-2 to 3-5) drop the main plot.** No briefing or victory text mentions the Choir until the 3-6 boss. Sector 2 worked because every mission moved the Vex hunt along. Sector 3 reads as a string of mechanic lessons, and my motivation dipped noticeably.
2. **Victory text and cutscenes tell the same reveal two to four times.** This happens at 1-6 → s1 (commission, Lamp 9, the Belt) and at 2-6 → s2 (the Choir explained in the victory text, then by Vex, her log and Mox). 3-6 → s3 is smaller but similar. The reveal loses its punch. Lamp 9's history appears only in the 1-6 victory paragraph, which is also the only place the game says the Lamps "came back on" after the Starfall.
3. **Story text depends on optional Elites, or contradicts play.**
   - **2-4** mentions "the bounty on your head", which is set up only in the optional 2-E.
   - **Mox** is introduced twice (2-E2 and 2-3).
   - **3-4's victory** claims the Alpha went down "in one blow" after a fight of several turns.
   - **The Hivewarden briefing** mentions "the Swarm Wars" once, unexplained.
4. **Difficulty spikes at the start of each sector and at the 3-6 boss.**
   - **2-1** took me to 7 Health twice.
   - **3-1** was my first loss, made worse by autofill giving me no area damage for a Swarm sector.
   - **Ixis on Officer** healed to full twice and flooded the board.

   The Cadet retry offered after one boss loss is an excellent safety net and kept this persona playing.
5. **The Vex/Ixis endings work emotionally but are now one template, and the Legendary rewards undercut them.** Both are sympathetic: they "said yes" and the Choir spoke through them. Both die, and both get confirmed dead in a cutscene. Then I'm handed each one as a card to deploy. Vex's death is now clear in the cutscene. The victory text ("it took her") is still vague, and so is my responsibility for her death.

## Suggestions (opinion)
- Give each of 3-2 to 3-5 one line of Choir connection, for example the hum in the nebula, Swarm moving "as one", or Okonkwo noticing the brood acting out of character.
- Keep victory text to the immediate aftermath (one or two sentences) when a cutscene follows, and let the cutscene own the reveal. Move Lamp 9's history (the Lamps came back on, then the Fleet shut Lamp 9 down) into the s1 cutscene.
- Either set up the bounty in a story mission (a line in 2-3) or move Mox's 2-4 line to after Kade.
- Rewrite 3-4's victory text so it fits a multi-turn fight, or give the Alpha a mechanic that makes "one blow" mean something.
- Add one line explaining why a dead captain or queen becomes my card (her crew or brood swearing to me, her flagship salvaged).
- Have the Sector 3 briefing (or the deck-size-up message) suggest area damage before 3-1, and make autofill favour sector counters.
- Replace "since February" with an in-world time ("for eight months").

## Harness issues (not the game)
- **Victory text lost on a mid-chain win, and no way to get it back.** In bash, an unquoted `#34` starts a comment, so the rest of my command line was silently dropped. I had to quote unit tags ('#34'). Separately, at 2-1 I won partway through a chained command whose output I was hiding, so I missed the 2-1 victory text. Replaying the mission shows only "+15 Credits (replay reward)" and not the story text, so I couldn't recover it. The 2-1 victory text is the one line I never read. (A story log or Archive page for past victory texts would also help real players.)
- **Silent errors in chains.** When an attack is blocked by a Guardian, or a targeted card is played without a target, the error is easy to miss if output is filtered. At 3-1 this cost me two attacks. That's my filtering, not the game's, but the error wording ("Usage: attack <attacker> <target>…") is generic when a tag refers to a dead unit.
- **Command naming.** `open academy` fails because it needs the tier number (`open 1`). `deck add void_plunderer` fails because the id is `plunderer`, though the display name works.
- **No Anchorage command.** I collected 670 Alloy with no way to spend it in the harness, so I can't judge Anchorage.
- **Stale Advisor tips** (these may be game issues):
  - The Energy Cell tip appeared when I was going first (2-1 replay).
  - The Ixis tip warned about an enemy Lamprey Sentinel when only *I* had one.
  - The turn-1 Advisor tip stayed on screen after I followed it (1-1).
- **Minor:** the Twin Pistols flavour text shows nested quotes (`"Sable: "With two guns…`). It's probably the game's string formatting.
