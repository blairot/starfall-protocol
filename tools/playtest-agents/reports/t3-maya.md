# Playtest report: Maya (card-game newcomer) · t3-maya · build 65 · 2026-10-20

## Persona
Maya, 29, nurse. Plays match-3 and cosy sims on the bus in 10-15 minute bursts. Has heard of Hearthstone, never played a card game. Default difficulty (Officer). Reads a tip the first time, then skims. Plays by feel: deploys whatever she can afford, hits the enemy commander's face, rarely plans ahead. Quits after two losses in a row on one mission, or after a loss she doesn't understand.

## Expectations (before playing)
"Space story with a sarcastic robot teacher. I like the robot already ('Most cadets fail. I mention this purely for context.'). I hope it's a game I can play in short bits on the bus and it explains things as it goes, not a rulebook up front. The first screen telling me my deck is 13/15 and *must* be 15 made me nervous for a second. I don't know what to put in it yet."

## Session summary
- Played Fleet Academy 1-1 to 1-6 in order on Officer (default). **6 wins, 0 losses.** No retries.
- Skipped Elite 1-E (Maya read the briefing and "Most cadets do not get through this one" put her off; she'd rather keep a perfect run).
- After graduating I watched cutscene s1, opened the free Academy Pack, bought and opened a Scrap Belt Pack with the 125 graduation Credits, added Okonkwo, Dreadnought and Rhino Tank to the deck, used autofill to reach 20, glanced at the Archive (codex) and read the 2-1 briefing.
- Time equivalent: about 6 to 8 minutes per battle (8 or 9 turns each), roughly 50 minutes total including menus. Each battle fits one bus ride, which is good.

## Critical incidents (chronological)

- **[Start screen] confusion.** The first status screen says "Deck: 13/15 cards << the deck must have exactly 15 to launch". The 1-1 briefing then says "This simulation uses a standard training deck." and launch just worked. I expected to be blocked and to have to fix something I didn't understand. *Minor.*
- **[1-1, turn 1] polish.** The Advisor said "If a card in your hand costs 1, click it to deploy it. Then press End Turn" and the same tip stayed up after I'd already spent my Energy. Tips in general stayed on screen for a whole battle (1-3's "Only Guardians block…" was shown on all 9 turns). After the first read I stopped looking at the Advisor at all. *Minor.*
- **[1-1, victory] boredom / surprise.** The victory screen was a card list: "New cards: Plasma Bolt, Rifle Squad, Recon Officer, Medic Bot. HALCYON has loaded them into your deck…, swapping out Fleet Cadet, Scout Drone." No line from HALCYON, no "well done". The same was true for 1-1 to 1-5. Only the boss win had a voice line. Wins felt flat. *Minor.*
- **[1-1 to 1-5, rewards] confusion.** Every briefing promised "Reward: a supply crate of new cards", but I never opened or chose anything. Cards just appeared, and the game removed others (it took my Plasma Bolt out after 1-4, which was my favourite card). I expected a box to open. *Minor.*
- **[1-2, turn 1] surprise.** Plasma Bolt ("Deal 2 damage.") could be aimed at my own commander and my own units. I thought spells only went at enemies, and it made me worried I'd zap myself by misclicking. *Minor* (the real game may prevent misclicks).
- **[1-2, turn 4] delight.** The tip "Use spells on enemy units that your own units can't beat in a fair fight" came the turn the enemy had a Rifle Squad, and I had a Plasma Bolt. I used it and felt smart. Great timing.
- **[1-3, turn 3] surprise (useful).** "Only Guardians block. Your other units do not stop enemies attacking your commander." This was news to me. I'd assumed my units stood in front of me. It's an important rule, and I only learned it on mission 3. *Minor.*
- **[1-3, turn 3] clarity win.** Trying to hit the face got "Can't attack that. Valid targets: E2 (a Guardian must be attacked first)". It's clear, and HALCYON's "locked door" line stuck with me.
- **[1-3, turn 9] delight.** I remembered "Spells ignore Guardian. A Plasma Bolt can still hit the commander directly." and finished the Fortress with a Bolt over its Guardian. Best moment of the session.
- **[1-4 briefing] confusion / boredom.** Three NEW boxes arrived at once: Commander Power, Opening Hand & Energy Cell, and Fatigue (the Fatigue one is a long paragraph about 1, then 2, then 3 damage). I skimmed all three. I never went second, so I never saw an Energy Cell, and no game got near Fatigue. I also had to choose a power ("power set") with no idea which was better. I picked Pulse Laser because it hurts things. *Minor-major for onboarding.*
- **[1-4, whole battle] observation.** I won 28/30 while barely using my power or my Guardians. Attacking the face every turn worked in all six battles. The Advisor's "Destroying enemy units keeps your commander safe" never mattered, because I never felt in danger before the boss. *Major (for teaching): the game never made me learn when to fight units instead.*
- **[1-5 briefing] confusion.** Warp: "Can attack the same turn it is deployed." Blitz: "Can attack enemy units (not commanders) the turn it is deployed." These are two space-sounding words for nearly the same idea. I had to reread them, and in battle I tried to remember which one could hit the face. In the hand listing, "Hover Tank … Blitz" doesn't say what Blitz does. *Minor.*
- **[1-5, turn 8] delight.** I was 1 damage short after my Heavy Trooper hit, used my Pulse Laser on the commander, and won. That's when the commander power clicked for me.
- **[1-6 briefing] confusion.** "Admiral Okonkwo is watching from the gallery." I had never heard of her. The first time she's mentioned, she's watching me. *Polish.*
- **[1-6, turns 7-8] tension (good).** HALCYON's board filled up, my Star Cruiser and Frigate got chewed up, and I dropped to 13. It was the first time I worried. It still felt fair, because I could see every hit.
- **[1-6 victory + cutscene s1] delight.** "You're the first this year to pass my final exam without crying. I'm counting the shouting separately." made me laugh. The distress call ("The dead Lamp out here is humming") gave me a reason to keep going. The story finally showed up here.
- **[Graduation screen] overwhelm.** In one screen I got Credits, Alloy, an Academy Pack, a Legendary, a new power, "Deck size increased to 20. Add 5 cards", a new pack set, and a paragraph about Supply Depot, Crafting/Scrap and Anchorage, plus a cutscene prompt. On a bus I'd skip all of that and probably not come back to it. *Major (onboarding).*
- **[Deck after graduation] relief / mild confusion.** I had to add 5 cards before I could play. I added the shiny ones (Okonkwo, Dreadnought, Rhino Tank) and then found "autofill", which saved me. If it weren't there I'd have been stuck on what "a good deck" is. *Minor.*
- **[Packs] delight.** Opening a pack and seeing "RARE NEW: Dreadnought 8/8 Guardian" was exciting. The second pack had "Cloak", a word I'd never seen. I had to look it up with `card`. *Polish.*
- **[1-E briefing] deterrent.** "Most cadets do not get through this one." Maya skips optional hard content after a line like that, so the Elite's reward never got a chance to tempt me. *Polish.*
- **[2-1 briefing] note.** Captain Vex is name-dropped and another new mechanic (Gear) arrives straight after the graduation info dump.

## Quit points
None. I never lost, so the two-losses rule never triggered. The closest was the graduation reward screen: if I were on a bus with a minute left, I'd close the app there and might not remember what Anchorage or Scrap were the next day.

## Debrief answers
1. **How do you win?** Get the other commander's Health to zero. Three things I learned: (a) Energy goes up by one each turn, and cards cost Energy, so big ships come later. (b) Guardians are walls: you have to knock them down before you can hit anything else, but spells fly over them. (c) Units can't attack the turn they arrive, except Warp ones (which can hit anything) and Blitz ones (only other units). Bonus: your power button costs 2 and you can use it once a turn. It won me 1-5.
2. **Story so far:** The space lanes run on alien "Lamps" that all switched off 70 years ago and killed loads of people (the Starfall). Now there's a signal from the middle of the galaxy, and a dead Lamp in the Scrap Belt is humming. I'm a cadet trained by HALCYON, a snarky AI. I passed, Admiral Okonkwo gave me a ship (the Meridian), and I'm off to investigate. A raider called Captain Vex is coming up.
3. **Best:** finishing the Fortress with a Plasma Bolt over its Guardian (1-3). **Worst:** the graduation wall of text. **Most confusing:** the 1-4 briefing with three rules at once, and Warp vs Blitz.
4. **Difficulty:** 1-1, 1-2 and 1-4 were easy, 1-3 and 1-5 about right, and the boss was the first fight where I felt any danger. No spikes. 1-4 was a dull dip, because its new thing (powers) didn't matter to winning.
5. **New cards:** I got them after every win, which is frequent and nice. But they just appeared in my deck (and some of my cards disappeared). It didn't feel like a reward until the actual pack opening after graduation, which felt great.
6. **Cutscenes/story:** The opening and graduation cutscenes added a lot, and HALCYON is the best part. Between them, missions 1-1 to 1-5 had almost no story: one quip in each briefing and nothing on winning.
7. **Play again tomorrow?** Yes, probably. I want to see what's humming, and opening packs is fun. What would stop me: the next session starts with that pile of Credits/Scrap/Alloy/Anchorage stuff I didn't read, or losing to the new raider mechanics twice without knowing why.
8. **To a friend:** "It's a space card game with a sarcastic AI teacher that teaches you one thing at a time, and I won every match just by hitting the face."

## Top 5 findings for the designers (most important first)
1. **Hitting the face won every battle on default difficulty (6-0, lowest Health 13/30).** The Academy never pushed Maya to fight units, use Guardians defensively, or plan ahead. The lessons about trading ("Destroying enemy units keeps your commander safe") were never tested, so she reached Sector 2 without the skill. Only the boss produced any tension.
2. **The graduation screen is an information dump.** One screen brings Credits, Alloy, a pack, a Legendary, a power, "Add 5 cards", a new pack set, a paragraph on Supply Depot, Crafting/Scrap and Anchorage, and a cutscene. A newcomer skims all of it. Anchorage and Scrap were never explained in a way she'd retain.
3. **The 1-4 briefing teaches three mechanics at once** (Commander Power, Opening Hand & Energy Cell, Fatigue). None of them came up in a way she noticed (she never went second and never got near Fatigue). The power choice ("pick a power in the Hangar") came with no guidance.
4. **Rewards in 1-1 to 1-5 don't feel like rewards.** Briefings promise "a supply crate of new cards", but no crate is opened or chosen. Cards are auto-loaded, existing cards are silently swapped out (Plasma Bolt was removed after 1-4), and victory screens have no HALCYON line. Wins felt flat compared with the single real pack-opening after graduation.
5. **Advisor tips go stale, and an important rule arrives late.** Tips stay on screen for the whole battle (1-3's tip ran all 9 turns; 1-1's "click a 1-cost card" stayed up with 0 Energy), so she stopped reading the Advisor. Meanwhile "your other units do not stop enemies attacking your commander" (no blocking) is first said on mission 1-3, and it overturned her picture of the game.

Also noted (lower priority): Plasma Bolt can target your own commander and units; Warp and Blitz are easy to mix up; Admiral Okonkwo is first mentioned as already "watching from the gallery"; "Cloak" appears in a pack before it's taught; the opening status screen says the deck "must have exactly 15" when 1-1 uses a training deck anyway; the Elite briefing's "Most cadets do not get through this one" puts newcomers off.

## Suggestions (opinion)
- Make one Academy mission punish pure face-attacking. For example, the enemy races you, so ignoring its units loses. Then have the defeat or Advisor say *why*.
- Spread graduation out: show the pack and Legendary first, then introduce Supply Depot, Anchorage and Crafting one at a time on the next visits or missions.
- Move Fatigue and the Energy Cell to the mission where they first matter, or show them as an in-battle tip when they happen.
- Put a one-line HALCYON quip on each victory screen, and show the "crate" as a short reveal, even if the cards are fixed.
- Clear or change the Advisor tip once its advice has been followed, and say "units don't block" in 1-1.
- Show a short reminder for keywords in the hand (e.g. "Blitz: attack units on arrival").

## Harness issues (text interface, not the game)
- No command to visit Anchorage or spend Alloy, so I couldn't try the third unlocked feature.
- No mulligan prompt in 1-1 to 1-3 (it started in 1-4 when the Opening Hand rule was taught). This may be intended by the game, not the harness.
- Flavour text on Okonkwo shows nested quotes: `"Okonkwo: "I don't lose positions…` Probably harness formatting, but worth checking in the real card.
- Otherwise the harness worked smoothly: clear error on illegal attacks, `look`/`card`/`codex` all helpful.
