# Playtest report: Sam (the collector) · t2-sam · build 63 · 2026-10-20

## Persona
Sam, 23, student. Plays gacha and collectible games for the packs, the set-completion bars and the "one more pull" feeling. Officer difficulty (the default), decent skill. Opens packs immediately, checks the collection all the time, does every optional Elite, and replays missions if farming pays.

## Expectations (before playing)
The prologue sells a sci-fi card battler with a sarcastic AI teacher. My hope: lots of packs early, a collection screen with a big "X / Y collected" bar, rarity reveals that feel good, and a currency that lets me buy what I want. My worry: tutorial games often hold the fun stuff back for an hour.

## Session summary
- **Played:** all of Sector 1 (1-1 to 1-6 plus Elite 1-E) and all of Sector 2 (2-1 to boss 2-6 plus both Elites, 2-E2 and 2-E). Also two farming replays (1-1 and 2-1).
- **Battles:** 19 in total. 17 wins, 2 losses: the first try at Elite 2-E2 Salvage Rights and the first try at boss 2-6 Captain Vex. I won both on the second attempt after rebuilding my deck.
- **Time:** most battles ran 8 to 13 turns, and several went to fatigue. In the real game I'd guess about 2 to 2.5 hours.
- **End state:** 64 cards (47 different), 2 Legendaries (Okonkwo, Vex), 4 Epic copies (Shipbreaker Titan, Gun Tinker, Cluster Cannon x2), 70 Credits, 55 Scrap, 430 Alloy, 4 commander powers, deck size 25, Hive Pack tier unlocked.

- **No hard quit.** But Sam would have been at real risk around **2-3 to 2-5**. ## Debrief answers
1. **How do you win?** Get the enemy commander to 0 Health. You get one more Energy each turn, play units and spells, and units fight by dealing their Attack to each other. Three things I learned: **Guardian** (enemies must hit those units first, which is how I finally beat Vex), **Warp/Blitz** (Warp hits anything, including the commander, the turn it lands; Blitz only hits units), and **Gear** (your commander attacks with a weapon but takes damage back, and each swing uses Durability). Bonus: **Salvage** triggers on death, and **Broadside**-style area damage hits Cloaked units.
2. **Story so far:** I'm a new Fleet commander. HALCYON, the snarky training AI, passed me, and Admiral Okonkwo gave me the frigate Meridian and the station Anchorage. A dead Lamp in the Scrap Belt started humming. Captain Vex, the raider queen, was taken over by "the Choir", a voice from the galactic core that promises you what you want. Mox (her ex-salvage boss) and Sable helped me reach her. Now the Choir is after the Swarm in the Hive Nebula.
3. **Best moment:** winning Iron Wall at 2 Health and getting the Dreadnought I'd just fought. **Worst:** spending my first 125 Credits on a pack with three Heavy Rail Rifles. **Most confusing:** Elite Salvage Rights blowing up in my face before the game had taught Salvage.
4. **Difficulty:** smooth through Sector 1, with the Elite as a good optional spike. Sector 2 was mostly "right". The two spikes were the Salvage Elite (unlocked too early) and Vex (fair once I added Guardians). Kade was oddly easy for an Elite. The dull stretch was 1-2 to 1-5: long games against rising Health with all-common rewards.
5. **New cards:** frequent in Sector 1 but not exciting (auto-added commons). In Sector 2 they came less often but were more exciting: Elite rewards and crate choices were good, packs were a coin flip and often duplicates. Rares and Epics did feel worth playing (Dreadnought, Titan, Cluster Cannon, Void Plunderer all made my deck and won games). Commons from packs mostly went straight to Scrap.
6. **Cutscenes:** short and fine. HALCYON's lines are funny. Vex's log ("I said yes.") landed. They didn't get in my way.
7. **Play again tomorrow?** Yes, for the Hive Pack and to see what Vex (my Legendary) does. But I'd quit fast if Sector 3 story missions also pay 10 Credits, and if I still can't see a set-completion bar or spend my Scrap.
8. **One sentence for a friend:** "Solid little card battler where the Elites and bosses shower you with Legendaries, but the everyday Credit drip is so slow that buying packs barely matters."

**Pacing numbers:**
- Credits per story mission in Sector 2: **10**. Replay reward: **15**. Elite: **40 + pack**. Boss: **60 + pack**.
- Credits from Sector 2 story missions: 50 for 5 missions, which is 40% of one pack.
- Elites and boss supplied 140 of my 220 Sector 2 Credits plus 3 of my 4 Sector 2 packs.
- Across 6 packs (30 cards): **15 new**, 7 second copies, **8 converted to Scrap (27%)**. 6 of those 8 were cards I'd already maxed out through x2 crate picks.
- "Time to next new thing": every battle in Sector 1 (all commons). In Sector 2, roughly every battle there was something (a crate or Elite drop), but a *pack* took about 3 to 4 battles if I did the Elites, and would have taken about 12 story missions without them.

## Top 5 findings for the designers (most important first)

1. **Sector 2's Credit economy is starved and inverted.** Story missions pay **10 Credits** against a **125-Credit** pack (about 12 to 13 missions per pack). Replaying an already-cleared mission (even 1-1, the 12-Health tutorial drone) pays **15 Credits**, more than any new Sector 2 story mission. Briefings only say "Reward: replay reward", so the farming math is hidden. Without the Elites, buying packs would barely exist as a loop.
2. **x2 crate picks poison later packs.** Every crate gives two copies, which hits the deck limit, so later pack copies of the same card go straight to Scrap. 6 of my 8 scrapped pack cards (Broadside x3, Harpoon Gun x2, Shadow Corsair) were crate picks. One bought pack contained the same rare (**Heavy Rail Rifle**) **three times**. The boss pack after Vex had **one** new card. Overall, 27% of pack cards were dead on arrival.
3. **The first ~7 battles have no reward moment.** Five briefings promise "a supply crate of new cards", but the reward is a silent auto-add of commons with no reveal or choice, and there's no currency until graduation. The 1-6 dump is great, but its 100-Credit bonus is 25 short of one pack, so the first visit to the Supply Depot is a tease.
4. **No progression meters for a collector, and two currencies with no sink in reach.** The collection shows "64 cards, 47 different" with no denominator or rarity breakdown. Scrap (55) and Alloy (430) accumulated for 12 battles and I never spent them. Part of this is the harness, which has no craft or Anchorage commands, but no screen I saw told me what they would buy or how far I was from anything.
5. **Content and rewards arrive before they're explained, and Elite difficulty is uneven.** Gear cards appear in the 2-1 crate (shown with no Durability) before Gear is taught in 2-2. Elite 2-E2 (all Salvage) unlocks after 2-1, but Salvage is taught in 2-3, and I lost my first try to it. Meanwhile Elite Kade ("Methodical") barely attacked and was the easiest fight of Sector 2. Related AI oddities: the Tollmaster shot its own commander with Pulse Laser, the Bastion "used Nanite Repair" with no effect, and HALCYON and Vex sat on their signature powers for many turns despite tips saying they fire every turn.

## Suggestions (opinion)
- Raise story-mission Credits (around 25 to 30), or make the first clear pay clearly more than replays. Show the replay amount in the brief ("Replay: 15 Credits").
- Make crates give one copy, or exclude maxed cards from pack rolls (at least for commons). At minimum, never put three copies of one rare in a single pack.
- Turn Sector 1 rewards into a real crate open, even if it's fixed: flip the cards one at a time with rarity colour. Give 25+ Credits at graduation so the first Depot visit buys a pack.
- Add "47 / N collected" per set and per rarity, and show what Scrap crafts and what Alloy upgrades cost on the wallet line.
- Gate Elite 2-E2 behind 2-3, and don't offer Gear in crates before 2-2.

## Harness issues (not the game)
- **Gear Durability shows as "undefined"** in hand, crate, pack and `card` listings ("Shock Baton [1 Energy, common gear 2/undefined gear]"). On the board it displays correctly ("Heavy Rail Rifle 5/2").
- **No commands for crafting, scrapping, Anchorage or contracts**, although the game's Codex describes all four. Scrap and Alloy can't be spent, so I couldn't test that part of the economy at all.
- **Card ids don't match card names** for some cards. `deck add void_plunderer` gives "No such card." (the id is `plunderer`), and the same goes for `rail_rifle` and `shipbreaker`. I had to use `collection <filter>` to find ids.
- **Hand and unit indices shift after every play or death**, which caused a couple of misplays when I chained commands (I played Shipbreaker Titan instead of Void Plunderer once). Probably fine for a text harness, but worth a warning in `help`.
- **Turn order display:** a few times the opening said "you go second", but my Turn 1 began before any visible enemy action (1-5, 2-3). Other times a "Before your first turn:" block appeared. I couldn't tell whether the enemy's first turn was empty or just not shown.
- **Tip mismatch, possibly the game's:** on the 2-1 replay I went first, but the Advisor said "Use the Energy Cell to play a 2-cost card on turn 1", and I had no Energy Cell.
- **"Academy Pack pack" / "Scrap Belt Pack pack"** and "+1 Scrap Belt Pack pack(s)": doubled word, from either the harness or the game's string.
- **My own mistake, not a harness bug:** I hid the output of my final attack in 1-3, so I didn't record its victory text. The rewards above for 1-3 come from the status and collection screens afterwards.
