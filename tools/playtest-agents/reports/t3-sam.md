# Playtest report: Sam (the collector) · t3-sam · build 65 · 2026-10-20

## Persona
Sam, 23, student, plays gacha and collectible games. Opens packs the second they land, checks the collection all the time, does every optional Elite, and replays missions if it pays. Officer difficulty (the default), decent skill. Watching reward pacing, packs and crates, duplicates, the Credits/Scrap/Alloy economy and how long it is until the next new thing.

## Expectations (before playing)
The prologue is a big "lost lanes, alien Lamps, Swarm coming back" setup, and then a snarky AI says most cadets fail. I'm expecting a Hearthstone-style campaign where the Academy is the tutorial and the real collection opens up afterwards. What I want: packs early, a reason to care about rarity, and a collection screen with a completion number I can grind toward. The first screen says "Supply Depot and Anchorage are locked until you graduate", so I already know the shop is the carrot.

## Session summary
- Played 1-1 to 1-6 (including Elite 1-E), then 2-1 to boss 2-6 (including Elites 2-E2 and 2-E). Replayed 2-1 and Elite 2-E2 to test farming.
- **17 battles, 17 wins, 0 losses** on Officer. Closest calls: 2-E2 replay (won on 5 Health) and 2-E2 first clear (won on 10 Health).
- Bought 3 packs and earned 4, so opened **7 packs (35 cards)**. Collection at the end: 68 cards, 50 different, 3 Legendaries.
- Time-equivalent: roughly 3.5 to 4 hours (most battles ran 9 to 11 turns; 1-4 ran 14 turns to fatigue).

## Critical incidents (chronological)

- **[1-1 victory] surprise.** The brief said "Reward: a supply crate of new cards". What actually happened: "New cards: Plasma Bolt, Rifle Squad, Recon Officer, Medic Bot. HALCYON has loaded them into your deck…". I expected to open a crate. Instead nothing opened, and I couldn't pick or see a rarity. All four were common. For a collector, a "crate" that never opens is a letdown. Severity: minor.
- **[1-1 to 1-5, every victory] frustration.** Every win in Sector 1 auto-swaps cards out of my deck ("swapping out Heavy Trooper, Patrol Frigate, Plasma Bolt"). After 1-E I had hand-built a deck around my new Dreadnought and Bulwark Mech. 1-5's reward then removed Heavy Trooper, Patrol Frigate and Plasma Bolt that I had deliberately added. It kept the Dreadnought, but my edits were overwritten without asking. I understand it's teaching, but it fights the collector urge to curate. Severity: minor.

## Debrief answers
1. **How do you win?** Get the enemy commander to 0 Health. Mechanics I learned: (a) Guardian units must be attacked first, so they protect what's behind them; (b) Cloak hides a unit from attacks and targeting until it attacks, but area damage like Cluster Cannon still hits it; (c) Salvage triggers when a unit dies, e.g. Mine Layer pings everything on your side, so killing it can hurt you. Also: Gear lets your commander attack, taking damage back when it hits units.
2. **Story so far?** HALCYON trains you at the Academy; Admiral Okonkwo gives you the frigate Meridian and the Anchorage station. In the Scrap Belt you cut through Captain Vex's raiders with help from Mad Mox, then find out Vex was taken over by "the Choir", a mind at the galactic core that offers people what they want. Next it's after the Swarm in the Hive Nebula.
3. **Best:** Aurora's deploy buff on Okonkwo and the Dreadnought for exact 16-damage lethal in 2-E2. **Worst:** the Vex boss pack giving zero new cards right after the climax. **Most confusing:** what Alloy and Scrap are actually for, since I had 440 Alloy and no idea what it buys.
4. **Difficulty:** gentle all the way, with no losses. The Elites were the hardest (2-E2 especially). There was a dull stretch at 1-4 (fatigue slog). Bosses felt about as hard as story missions.
5. **New cards:** Sector 1 was a drip of auto-loaded commons, with no rarity and no choice. Then 1-6 plus the first two packs was a firehose (2 Legendaries and 4 rares in about 3 minutes). By the end of Sector 2 the Scrap Belt set was mostly collected and packs were mostly duplicates. Meaningful? Yes: Okonkwo, Aurora, Dreadnought, Cluster Cannon and Ghost Operative all made my deck. Frequent enough? Too lumpy: famine, then feast, then duplicates.
6. **Cutscenes:** short and they didn't get in the way. The Sector 2 ending (Vex's log) was the first time the story made me curious beyond the loot.
7. **Play again tomorrow?** Yes, for the new Hive Pack set and to fit Vex into a 25-card deck. What would stop me: if Hive packs also turn into duplicates after about 4 packs, and if Scrap/Alloy have nowhere to go.
8. **To a friend:** "Solid little card campaign with real Legendaries early, but the pack economy runs out of new stuff fast."

## Top 5 findings for the designers (most important first)

1. **Set exhaustion: Scrap Belt packs stop giving new cards within about 6 packs.** New cards per Scrap Belt pack in order: 5, 3, 3, 1, 2, 0. The Elite (2-E) and boss (2-6) packs, which should feel like the biggest rewards, were 4/5 and 5/5 duplicates. The 2-E2 pack also re-gave the Shipbreaker Titan the same Elite had just awarded. Duplicates turn into Scrap only beyond 2 copies, and I couldn't find anything to spend the Scrap on.
2. **Credits pacing makes packs a 5-mission wait, and farming doesn't help.** Story missions pay 25 Credits; packs cost 125. Replays paid 15 Credits (story) and 30 Credits (Elite) with no pack, so a pack costs 4 to 8 full replays. I ended up 5 to 10 Credits short of a pack twice (115, 120). Elites (50 Credits + pack + named cards) were by far the best reward beats.
3. **Rarity is front-loaded right after graduation.** Sector 1's first 5 missions gave only auto-loaded commons with no choice. Then 1-6 plus the first Academy pack gave 2 Legendaries (Okonkwo, The Aurora) and a rare, and the first purchased pack had 3 rares. By the end of Sector 2 I owned 3 Legendaries. That was exciting once, but Legendary stopped feeling special early.
4. **Alloy and Scrap pile up with no visible use.** I ended with 440 Alloy and 55 Scrap. The codex describes Anchorage modules and crafting, but I never reached either through the text interface (see Harness issues). The game never prompted me to spend them. If the real game also lacks a nudge, players will hoard a currency they never see the point of.
5. **Crates are good when they're a real choice, and that was often not the case.** The choose-1-of-3 crates (2-1 to 2-4) are well presented ("· new" / "· you have 1"). But 2-3 offered 2 duplicates out of 3, 2-4 offered 1 duplicate, 2-5 had no crate, and every Sector 1 "supply crate" was auto-opened and auto-slotted with no choice. On top of that, the collection screen never shows a completion count ("50 / N"), the progression meter collectors look for.

## Suggestions (opinion)
- Make Elite and boss packs guarantee at least 1 or 2 cards you don't own (or "new if any remain" logic). Don't let a pack re-roll a card the same mission just awarded.
- Show set completion on the collection screen and in the Depot (e.g. "Scrap Belt: 22/26").
- Replace "Reward: replay reward" with the actual numbers. Consider making the first replay of an Elite each day worth a pack, or a 25-Credit floor, so farming is a valid choice.
- Let Sector 1 crates show their contents and rarity, even if HALCYON still auto-loads them. Stop overwriting manual deck edits, or ask first.
- After the first Alloy reward, add a one-time prompt pointing to Anchorage, and show "next module: X Alloy".
- Spread the post-graduation Legendaries out (e.g. no Legendary in the guaranteed first Academy pack).

## Harness issues (text interface, not the game)
- `help` lists no command for **crafting** or for **Anchorage / spending Alloy**, so I couldn't test either. Alloy (440) and Scrap (55) were unspendable for me. I can't tell whether the real game exposes them.
- Unit slot numbers (`A1`, `E2`…) shift as soon as a unit dies, which caused two misplays (attacked with the wrong unit after an earlier attack killed something). That's specific to the text interface.
- `card heavy_rail_rifle` returned "No such card" because the id is `rail_rifle`, and `deck add void_plunderer` failed because the id is `plunderer`. Ids don't always match names, so I had to look them up with `collection <filter>`.
- A failed `play` (card not affordable) is silent if output is suppressed. My mistake, but a clearer error would help.
- The victory/reward block prints only after the final attack, so it's easy to miss (I missed the 1-6 victory text the first time and had to rebuild the rewards from `status`).
