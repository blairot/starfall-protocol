# Playtest report: Dev (the veteran) · t2-dev · build 63 · 2026-10-20

## Persona
Dev, 34, software engineer, Hearthstone Legend, years of Magic. Started on **Admiral** (hardest). Skips tutorial text, reads card text closely, builds every deck by hand, counts lethal, holds removal. Here to find out whether there is real depth, whether the AI can be exploited, and whether Admiral is actually hard.

## Expectations (before playing)
The opening is a Hearthstone-style lane/hero setup with a snarky training AI. I expect Hearthstone-lite: mana curve, a hero power, taunt, charge. What I want to know is whether "Admiral: tougher, smarter enemies" means the AI plays well or just gets more health. With 15-card decks I'm worried the decks will be too thin for any real deck-building.

## Session summary
- Played every mission in Sector 1 (1-1 to 1-6 plus Elite 1-E) and Sector 2 (2-1 to 2-6 plus Elites 2-E2 and 2-E), in story order, all on Admiral.
- Record: **15 wins, 1 loss.** The only loss was the first attempt at the Sector 2 boss, Captain Vex. I won the retry with 3 Health left.
- Sector 1: 7-0, never lower than 11 Health. Sector 2: 8-1. Things only got tense from 2-E (Kade) onwards.
- I rebuilt or tuned the deck before almost every battle. I used Pulse Laser as my commander power the whole way.
- Time-equivalent: about 3 to 3.5 hours of real play. Battles run 9 to 12 turns.

## Critical incidents (chronological)

- **[1-1 to 1-3, launch] SURPRISE / minor.** Academy missions 1-1 to 1-3 skip the mulligan entirely. The mulligan only appears from 1-4, when it's taught. Fine for a tutorial, but on Admiral I'd still like it. Severity: polish.
- **[After every Academy win] FRUSTRATION / major (for me).** "HALCYON has loaded them into your deck for the next lesson." Each win silently rewrote my hand-built deck. My second Marine, second Lancer and second Heavy Trooper got pulled for the new lesson cards. I had to re-tune before 1-3, 1-4, 1-5 and 1-6. For a deck-builder that's hostile. At minimum say "HALCYON swapped out X, Y, Z". Severity: major for veterans, probably right for newcomers.
- **[1-2, Plasma Bolt] CONFUSION / minor.** The card text is just "Deal 2 damage." It doesn't say to what. It can target my own commander and my own units. Rifle Squad says "to an enemy" and Sapper says "to an enemy unit". Those two are precise, which makes Bolt, Railgun ("Deal 4 damage.") and Pulse Laser ("Deal 1 damage.") look sloppy next to them. Severity: minor.
- **[1-2] AI MISTAKE.** The AI on Admiral spent Rifle Squad's ping on my full-health Colonial Marine. It bolted my face instead of my units and emptied its hand by turn 5. Severity: major for the "smarter enemies" claim.
- **[1-3, card text] POLISH.** Guardian cards render as "Guardian. **Guardian**", so the keyword appears twice. The Blast Barricade flavour text has broken quotes: `"Marine: "It was the best squadmate...`. Okonkwo's has the same problem. These may be harness rendering. Later in the run, after the output format changed, they showed once.
- **[1-4] AI.** Vale was introduced as having a commander power and never used it. The 1-E Bastion only used Nanite Repair once it was nearly dead. Its 8 Armor was gone by turn 6.
- **[1-E, turn 11] SURPRISE / minor.** Fatigue exists: "You ran out of cards: 1 fatigue damage", then 2. It isn't in the codex or any tip. With 15-card decks, turn 11 to 12 fatigue is a real factor and card draw (Supply Drop, Recon, Plunderer) has a hidden cost. Players should be told. Severity: minor.
- **[1-E] DIFFICULTY.** The Elite briefing says "Most cadets do not get through this one." I won comfortably with 20 HP and was never in danger. Severity: major (difficulty claim vs reality).
- **[Repeated, mulligans] OBSERVATION.** Four times my mulliganed 5-drop (Frigate, Bulwark Mech) came straight back. Each time I had 2 copies, so it may be variance. Worth checking that a mulliganed card can't be redrawn as the replacement. Severity: unknown.
- **[2-1 start] CONFUSION / polish.** My max Health became 32/32 in Sector 2 with no message saying why.
- **[2-1 crate] CONFUSION / major.** The crate offered Shock Baton, Plasma Cutlass and Harpoon Gun before Gear had been introduced. It showed "2/undefined gear", so I couldn't evaluate durability. 2-2's crate did the same with Salvage before Salvage was introduced in 2-3's brief. The "undefined" is likely a harness rendering bug (in play it showed "2/2", and later output was fixed). Offering cards with untaught keywords is a game issue either way.
- **[2-2, Rourke] AI MISTAKE.** He used Arm Blade to equip a Combat Knife, then immediately played Shock Baton, overwriting it. He wasted 2 Energy plus the knife.
- **[2-2] DELIGHT.** Harpoon Gun is a 2-mana 2/2 weapon with a 1-damage ping. It's excellent and possibly undercosted. I played 2 copies in every deck after this.
- **[2-3, turn 2 Advisor] CONFUSION / minor.** "When Mine Layer dies, it deals 1 damage to everything on your side." I had Mine Layer in my own hand, and its text says "Deal 1 damage to all enemies". The tip is written from the enemy's point of view without saying so. Similarly, "all enemies" vs "all enemy units" (Broadside) is a precise distinction, but nothing explains that "enemies" includes the commander. I found out when my Mine Layer hit Ott's face.
- **[2-3] DELIGHT.** Cluster Cannon plus a commander swing into a 1/1 cleared a whole board of drones. Broadside killed three units and their Salvage chain. AoE feels powerful and correct against this sector.
- **[2-E2 / 2-E / 2-4] EXPLOIT: Dreadnought freezes the AI. Major.** Every time I had Dreadnought (8/8 Guardian) on board, the opponent skipped attacking entirely. Breaker Jun passed a whole turn with seven 1/1s. Kade passed two turns. Sable's cloaked units sat idle for three turns. None. The real risk was the Academy: six missions with no decisions on Admiral. If I hadn't been assigned the run, I'd have been close to quitting around 1-4 ("this is the hardest difficulty?"). Sector 2 pulled me back in.

## Debrief answers
1. **How do you win?** Reduce the enemy commander to 0. Three mechanics: (a) **Guardian**: enemies must attack Guardians first, though spells ignore it. (b) **Salvage**: death triggers, so killing a Scrap Golem gives the enemy two 1/1s, and AoE is how you beat it. (c) **Gear**: commander weapons with durability, and the commander takes damage back when hitting units. Cluster Cannon turns a commander swing into a board ping. Also Warp (charge), Blitz (can only hit units the turn it arrives), Cloak (untargetable until it attacks) and fatigue.
2. **Story?** Seventy years after the Lamps (warp-lane machines) went dark, a signal from the galactic core is waking things up. I graduated from HALCYON's Academy, got the frigate Meridian, and went to the Scrap Belt where Lamp 9 is humming. Raider queen Captain Vex had been taken over by "the Choir", which promises people what they want. Mad Mox (Vex's salvage boss) turned informant, and Okonkwo runs the Fleet side. Next stop: the Hive Nebula and the Swarm. HALCYON's dry lines were the best writing.
3. **Best:** the Vex retry. Broadside killing five units including a Det-Bot, then exact lethal at 3 HP. **Worst:** the Academy on Admiral, where the AI threw away cards for six missions. **Most confusing:** the 2-1 crate offering "2/undefined gear" for a mechanic I hadn't been taught.
4. **Difficulty curve:** flat and trivial through Sector 1 (the Elite included). It rose at 2-E2 and 2-E, then 2-4, 2-5 and 2-6 were genuinely tight (11 to 14 HP left). The Vex spike is fine. The early flatness on Admiral is the problem.
5. **New cards:** frequent enough. Sector 2 crates and packs were meaningful: Harpoon Gun, Broadside, Cluster Cannon and Void Plunderer all reshaped my deck. Credits are tight (I was 5 short of a 125-Credit pack twice). Alloy (410) and Scrap (425) piled up with nothing to spend them on in this interface.
6. **Cutscenes:** short and they stay out of the way. HALCYON's voice is good. I skimmed most briefings. I wouldn't have missed them, but they didn't hurt.
7. **Play again tomorrow?** Yes, for Sector 3 and to see whether the Hive AI is as sharp as Vex. What would stop me: if every non-boss opponent stays as passive against a big Guardian as they were here.
8. **To a friend:** "Hearthstone-lite single-player with surprisingly good boss AI, but even on hardest the first sector is a cakewalk and a big Guardian breaks most of the AI."

## Top 5 findings for the designers
1. **Admiral isn't hard until late Sector 2.** I went 7-0 in Sector 1 and never dropped below 11 HP, including the "most cadets do not get through this" Elite (won with 20 HP). The Academy AI threw away burn and pings, emptied its hand early, and some commanders never used their power. Non-boss Admiral opponents feel like Officer with more Health. Only Captain Vex (and partly Kade and Wreck Run) played at a level that matched the label.
2. **A large Guardian freezes most of the AI (exploit).** With Dreadnought 8/8 Guardian on board, Breaker Jun, Kade and Sable each skipped whole attack phases, with up to seven units idle. Dreadnought is also a Sector 1 Elite reward, so it's available early. Only Vex had an answer (Broadside, then Volley to enable Shakedown). The AI needs to chip or swarm Guardians, or attack face with Warp/Cloak units when a Guardian blocks the rest.
3. **The Academy rewrites the player's deck.** "HALCYON has loaded them into your deck" silently removed cards I had chosen, after every Academy win. That's fine for newcomers, hostile for deck-builders. At least list what changed, or skip it once the player has edited their deck manually.
4. **Mechanics show up in rewards before they're taught, and some rules are never taught.** Gear cards were offered in the 2-1 crate (durability shown as "undefined") before 2-2 introduced Gear. Salvage cards were offered in 2-2 before 2-3 introduced Salvage. Fatigue exists but isn't in the codex or any tip, and with 15 to 20 card decks it matters from turn 11. The Advisor's line "Once a Cloaked unit attacks, it is revealed for good" is contradicted by Smoke Screen.
5. **Card text precision is inconsistent.** Rifle Squad says "to an enemy" and Sapper says "to an enemy unit", but Plasma Bolt, Railgun Shot and Pulse Laser just say "Deal N damage." and can hit your own side. "All enemies" (Mine Layer) vs "all enemy units" (Broadside) is a meaningful split that's never explained. Kade's "Methodical... rarely hits you until lethal is close" doesn't match his 11-damage face turn. Also, a Scrap Belt Pack gave a duplicate of the Legendary (Vex) I'd been awarded minutes earlier, which may be a dupe-protection gap.

## Suggestions (opinion)
- On Admiral, give Academy opponents the same targeting logic Vex uses: save burn for units, use powers, don't empty the hand on turn 5.
- Teach the AI to attack into a Guardian when its combined attack can kill it, or to hold position less passively. Consider making Dreadnought a later reward.
- Add codex entries for Fatigue, Gear durability, Cloak plus re-cloak, and "enemies" vs "enemy units".
- Don't put cards with untaught keywords in crates. Or add a one-line keyword explanation on the crate screen.
- Harpoon Gun and Void Plunderer look slightly undercosted. Pulse Laser outclasses Nanite Repair and Launch Drone.

## Harness issues (text interface, not the game)
- **Gear stats showed "2/undefined gear"** in hand, collection, crate and `card` output for the first ~85% of the run. In play, gear showed correct durability ("Shock Baton 2/2"). Later output was fixed ("Harpoon Gun 2/2").
- **The output format changed mid-run.** "enemy"/"your" prefixes appeared, the doubled "Guardian. **Guardian**" disappeared, and "Academy Pack pack" became "Scrap Belt Pack". The early text-quality notes may be harness artefacts.
- **No Anchorage or crafting commands.** The codex says to spend Alloy at Anchorage and Scrap in the Hangar, but `help` has neither. I finished with 410 Alloy and 425 Scrap unspent, so I couldn't test that economy.
- **Unit indices shift as units die.** That's correct behaviour, but chaining several commands in one call caused me to misplay three times (wrong attacker or wrong card). That's my problem, not the game's.
- **Battle-ending output was hidden** when I truncated attack output. It's recoverable via `status` and `crate` (`crate x` lists the pending options).
- **Card draws aren't logged** ("You draw X"): only the deck counter changes, so Plunderer and Salvage draws are invisible until `look`.
- **Salvage triggers aren't labelled** in the log. Damage just appears ("Colonial Marine took 2 damage") with no "Det-Bot Salvage:" prefix. This could be the game's log too.
- **Mulligan doesn't report what was swapped in.**
