# Playtest report: Lee (the struggling player) · t6-lee · build 73 · 2026-10-21

## Persona
Lee, 52, coming back to games after years away. Slow to learn rules and makes honest mistakes: plays the biggest card that fits, trades badly, attacks into Guardians, forgets what Equip and Twin-Fire do. Started on Officer and switched to Cadet the first time the game offered it.

## Expectations (before playing)
"I'm starting halfway through. My deck was built for me and it's full of legendaries, so I hope I can just play my big cards and keep up. I've heard the late bosses are walls of armor, so I'm worried I'll get stuck with nothing to do."

## Session summary
- Start: `--from 4`, Officer, auto-built 30-card deck. Lee made a few swaps: added Fortify and a second Venom Graft (because the defeat screen said to bring draw), and later Storm Titan, Calculus Prime and Flash Freeze. Equipped the Data Mine power after Sector 5 because the defeat screen kept naming it.
- Played every story mission from 4-1 to 6-5 in order. Skipped all Elites; Lee wouldn't try them.
- **Result: finished the game.** 17 wins and 3 losses over 20 battles. Sectors 4 and 5 and 6-1 to 6-2 were on Officer. After losing 6-3 the game suggested Cadet, Lee took it, and 6-3, 6-4 and 6-5 were won on Cadet.
- About 6 to 7 hours of real play. Several Officer battles ran 18 to 23 turns.

## Critical incidents (chronological)

- **[4-1, T13] confusion · major.** Playing Vex says "Equip a 3/2 Plasma Cutlass". Lee thought the sword went on Vex. It went on Lee's commander, shown only as `gear Plasma Cutlass 3/2 [CAN ATTACK]` on the commander line. Lee never swung it, and the turn ended with the boss at 1 Health. Equipped gear isn't flagged as something you can attack with.
- **[4-1, T10] confusion · polish (may be harness).** Typing `power` without a target did nothing ("Your power needs a target"), and Lee didn't notice that the heal hadn't happened.
- **[4-2, T4] confusion · minor.** The briefing teaches "EMP: Wipes a unit". The battle log then says "your Scrap Rat was silenced". That's two words for one thing. Lee had to guess that "silenced" meant EMP.
- **[4-2, T10 to T19] frustration / unfair · blocker on first attempt.** This is the core focus question. From turn 10 Lee had 0 or 1 cards in hand every turn. The Jammer Array's armor went 4, 8, 17, 21, 25, 29, 31, 36, 41. Reactor Core gives it 2 Armor every turn, on top of its power. Lee's Health only went down. Lee conceded at 62 effective enemy Health against Lee's 6. **Yes: the late game still leaves a struggling player with an empty hand while the enemy keeps building, at least in 4-2.**
- **[4-2 defeat screen] delight + frustration · major.** The advice was specific and correct: "You spent 10 turns with one card or none in hand while the enemy kept building. Bring cards that draw (Supply Drop, Recon Officer, Fortify, Flash Freeze), or equip the Data Mine commander power if you have it." But at that point Lee owned no Fortify, no Flash Freeze and no Data Mine, and the Supply Drop and Recon Officer were already in the deck. The advice named cards Lee couldn't use. Lee added the second Venom Graft (it draws) instead. The retry still had a hand of 0 or 1 from turn 10 and was won by fatigue on turn 23, with Lee's deck at 0.
- **[4-2 retry, T14] surprise / AI · minor.** The enemy played EMP Trooper on its own Repair Drone.
- **[4-3, T7] frustration · major.** In one enemy turn Lee went from 25 to 13 (Repair Drone, Plating Tech and Heavy Trooper all went face past Lee's Titan). The next turn a Tri-Walker (5/6, Twin-Fire, Barrier) arrived, which is 10 damage a turn. Lee survived only by holding Kill Order. Without it this was a loss.
- **[4-4] delight.** Okonkwo's hint ("When it overheats... that's when you push") was clear. A clean, satisfying mission.
- **[4-6] surprise · minor.** The Sector 4 boss was easier than 4-2 and 4-3 (won in 12 turns and never dropped below 36 Health). The difficulty curve is upside down inside Sector 4.
- **[4-6 rewards] confusion · polish (harness).** `deck add calculus_prime` said "No such card". The id is `calculus`, and adding by name worked.
- **[5-2, T6 to T9] frustration then delight.** Cryo Sentries froze every unit Lee played, every turn ("Frozen" each time). Lee was at 11 with 3 frozen legendaries. Then two Acid Rains cleared all 7 enemy units. This was the best moment of the session.
- **[5-3, T5 to T16] unfair / confusion · major.** The enemy fielded Lee's own Vex, Shipbreaker Titan and Calculus Prime. Lee had no idea how. `card Telepath` explains it ("Copy a random card from your opponent's hand"), but the battle log only said the card was played. The defeat screen said "What hurt you most: Shipbreaker Titan 21 damage", which reads as if Lee's own Titan was the enemy. Its advice was the same draw-cards advice, even though Lee's hand was full for most of that game. The real problem (big cards stolen or copied) wasn't mentioned.
- **[5-3 retry, T10] surprise.** "Enemy Trap fired: Gravity Snare. your Storm Titan returned to hand." The Titan then cost 7 instead of 5. The reason was only visible through `card`.
- **[5-4, T8] unfair (in Lee's eyes) · minor.** Mirror Field copied Lee's Okonkwo onto the enemy board. The advisor had warned to "Bait traps with cheap units". Lee hadn't absorbed it until this happened. After that Lee baited properly (5-6: Venom Graft ate Psionic Null and a Blood Leech ate Mirror Field). The lesson did land, the hard way.
- **[5-4, T12] confusion / possible bug · minor.** The log said "Snare Mistress Ilu took 7 damage" when Kinetic Barrier fired, but her Health stayed at 31. The armor absorbed it. "Took 7 damage" while Health doesn't move looked broken to Lee.
- **[6-1 to 6-2] observation.** With Data Mine plus Supply Drop, Fortify and Flash Freeze, Lee's hand stayed at 6 to 9 cards in Sector 6. The empty-hand problem disappeared once Lee had the tools. The new problem was running out of deck: Lee's deck was at 2 to 4 cards by the end of 6-2 and 6-5.
- **[6-3 Officer] frustration · blocker (Lee's quit point without the Cadet offer).** Calculus Omega starts at 32 Health plus 6 to 7 Armor and was at 32 + 17 by turn 6. In 11 turns Lee never took a point off its Health. Every unit Lee played was killed or EMP'd the following turn (Kill Order on Okonkwo, Disruptor and EMP Blast on Calculus Prime, EMP Trooper on Ixis). Nanoforge kept making Barrier Nanobots. Hand size wasn't the problem: Lee held 7 to 9 cards. **On Officer, 6-3 was not passable for Lee.**

## Top 5 findings for the designers
1. **A struggling player can now finish the game, but only by dropping to Cadet at 6-3.** On Officer, Calculus Omega (32 Health plus up to 17 Armor, Nanoforge Barrier spam, repeated EMP and Kill Order on every threat) never lost a point of Health in 11 turns. On Cadet (21 + 6) it died on turn 8 with Lee untouched. 6-4 and 6-5 on Cadet were short and comfortable. The Cadet offer on the defeat screen is what kept Lee playing. The gap between Officer and Cadet at 6-3 is huge: impossible on one, trivial on the other.
2. **The empty-hand-while-the-enemy-builds problem still happens on Officer in 4-2, and the advice can't be acted on yet.** Both 4-2 attempts had 0 or 1 cards in hand from turn 10. Enemy armor kept climbing (Reconfigure plus Reactor Core) to 41. The retry was won only by deck-out. The defeat advice named Fortify, Flash Freeze and Data Mine, which Lee didn't own until later (Fortify from the 4-2 crate, Flash Freeze and Data Mine from the 5-6 rewards). Once Lee had them, hand size was fine in Sector 6. The fix to the problem exists, but it arrives after the place it's needed.
3. **The defeat screen is the best help the game gives, but it misreads some losses.** "One card or none in hand" was accurate in 4-2. In 5-3, though, the loss came from Telepath, Mirror Field and Mind Control copies of Lee's own legendaries, and the screen gave the same draw-cards advice and said "What hurt you most: Shipbreaker Titan". In 6-3 it named "Shield Drone 21 damage", which doesn't match what Lee saw. Wrong attribution teaches the wrong lesson.
4. **Stolen and copied units aren't explained when it happens.** Telepath ("copy a random card from your opponent's hand"), Mirror Field and Gravity Snare were only understandable through `card`. The log line "Enemy Trap fired: Mirror Field." didn't say "they now have a copy of your Admiral Okonkwo". To a struggling player it felt like cheating until Lee read the card text. The briefing and advisor warnings ("Bait traps with cheap units") were right. Lee only took them in after losing a legendary.
5. **Sector 4's difficulty is uneven, and gear on the commander is easy to miss.** 4-2 and 4-3 were harder than the 4-6 boss. Separately, Lee didn't realise "Equip a 3/2 Plasma Cutlass" put an attack on the commander, and finished 4-1's best turn 1 damage short. The commander line shows `gear ... [CAN ATTACK]`, but nothing points it out.

## Suggestions (opinion)
- Hand out Fortify or Data Mine in Sector 4, or point the 4-2 advice at cards the player actually owns.
- Make Reactor Core's armor gain, or the overall armor stacking, less punishing in 4-2 on Officer for players who reach it with the default deck.
- Add a step between Officer and Cadet for 6-3, for example a Cadet retry that keeps more Armor, or an Officer version with a lower Armor cap.
- When a card is copied or stolen, say so in the log: "Mirror Field: the enemy summons a copy of your Admiral Okonkwo."
- Use one word for EMP in the log ("EMPed", not "silenced").
- In "What hurt you most", attribute damage to the unit that dealt it, and say when it was a copy of your own card.
- Keep the 6-5 HALCYON +8 Health moment. It's excellent.

## Harness issues
- `power` with no target on a targeted power prints "needs a target" and does nothing. That's fine, but easy to miss when output is piped.
- `deck add calculus_prime` fails ("No such card"). The id is `calculus`, and adding by name works. Card ids don't always match the snake_case of the name.
- When several commands are chained, the victory, crate and phase-change text is easy to lose. A post-battle `crate` with no argument lists the pending choice, which helped.
- The 6-5 phase 1 to 2 transition wasn't obvious from the logs Lee read.
