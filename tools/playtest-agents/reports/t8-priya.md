# Playtest report: priya (story player) · t8-priya · Sectors 5–6 · 2026-10-27

## Persona
Priya, 41, teacher, plays Mass Effect and The Expanse for the characters. Officer difficulty, average skill: sensible play, no optimising. I read every briefing, victory text, cutscene, route event and Archive record.

## Expectations (before playing)
I started from the Sector 5 save, so I read the prologue and cutscenes s1 to s4 to catch up. By then the hook is strong: HALCYON's dry humour, Okonkwo learning the Fleet lied to her for fifty years, and "the Choir" as a voice that offers everyone what they want. I expected Nyra to be a tragic true believer, not a villain, and I expected HALCYON's mystery (why does a training AI care so much?) to pay off at the end. I hoped the finale would be about HALCYON.

## Session summary
- Sector 5: 5-1, 5-E2 (Elite), 5-2, 5-3, 5-4, 5-5, 5-E (Elite: **lost**, not retried), 5-6 Nyra. Cutscene s5.
- Sector 6: 6-1, 6-2, 6-3, 6-E (Elite: lost once, won on the retry), 6-E2 (Elite), 6-4, 6-5 finale (3 phases). Finale cutscene.
- Route events: all 9 that appeared (6 left over from Sectors 2–4 were still on the map at the start, plus ev-oracle, ev-vault and ev-beacon).
- Archive: read all 10 recovered records after upgrading the Archive to Mk 3.
- Packs: 1 Hive, 2 Void, 6 Singularity. Built new cards into the deck: Singularity set, Lampkeeper Sorrel, Nyra, HALCYON, Echo of the Lost Fleet.
- Proving Grounds: opened the intro and the start screen, then abandoned.
- Record: 13 wins and 2 losses, about 3.5–4 hours of real play at a human pace.

## Critical incidents (chronological)

- [Start, s4 cutscene] **confusion**: "Then it switched off every Lamp toward the core". At first I couldn't tell whether the signal or the Fleet switched them off. The Archive's Inquiry summary cleared it up later. Players who skip the Archive may stay confused. Severity: polish.
- [Start, map] **surprise**: All six route events from Sectors 2–4 were still waiting at Sector 5. Some read stale: in Drifting Freighter, Mox says to "leave before Vex's crews smell it", but Vex died two sectors ago. Severity: minor.
- [Start, first event choice] **surprise**: Resolving one event dumped 7 SERVICE RECORD lines at once (+350 Credits) for old achievements. It was jarring, as if they had been held back. Severity: polish.
- [Route event: Silent Foundry] **bug/confusion**: The option "Take a finished unit off the line (+1 card)" gave me **Fortify, a spell**. I expected a unit. Severity: minor.
- [Archive] **delight**: The Archive records are the best writing in the game. The New Lagos testimony ("I wish they wouldn't make it sound so pretty"), ANCHOR's transcript and the memo revealing HALCYON = ANCHOR all land. They also make the 5-3 HALCYON line about a "sealed sector" hum. Severity: none (praise).
- [Station] **surprise**: The "typical save" had 1,110 unspent Alloy and the Reactor at Mk 0. A real Priya would have upgraded long ago. Possibly a save-generator artefact. Severity: minor (harness/save).
- [5-1 brief] **delight**: HALCYON: "It sounds less like a threat than an invitation. I do not care for that." This builds the arc nicely.
- [5-E2 brief vs card] **confusion**: The brief says Sorrel guards "a Lamp the Orders switched back on last year". Her card says "I kept one Lamp dark for forty years. Now I keep the lights on for you." These can be reconciled, but it took me a moment. Also "the Orders" is never introduced before this point. Severity: polish.
- [5-E2, then 5-4] **confusion**: The optional Elite 5-E2 (available right after 5-1) throws two armed Traps at you. Traps are only formally taught ("NEW: Trap") in 5-4. Okonkwo gives nearly the same advice in both ("Play your cheap cards into the Traps first" / "Send in something cheap first"). Severity: minor.
- [5-2 brief] **polish**: "NEW: Cryo" appears in Sector 5, but Frost Lance (which Cryos) has been in my deck the whole time.
- [Lampkeeper Sorrel, The Archivist] **confusion**: Card text says "Your Protocols cost 1 less" and "add a random Protocol". Everywhere else, including the Amplify explanation, the word is "spells". The Codex says Protocols are spells, but the inconsistency made me double-check. Also, Psionic Null (a Trap) did not get Sorrel's discount, which I couldn't predict. Severity: minor.
- [5-2, 5-E2, 5-5] **frustration**: The Cryo-heavy Psionic decks froze my entire board 6+ times across Sector 5, often two turns in a row. Each fight then dragged to 12–17 turns. Severity: minor (pacing).
- [5-3, 5-E, 5-6] **unfair (perceived)**: Enemy decks Scan or Telepath-copy cards, so they played cards not in the Deep-Scan list (Kill Order, Chain Lightning, extra Mind Blasts). Once I understood it, it was fine. Before that, the Observatory's "full deck list" felt like it was lying. Severity: minor.
- [ev-oracle] **observation**: "Take her supplies and send her on" means robbing a refugee for +80 credits. It's a real moral choice, but it has no consequence or reaction, so it reads as a menu. Severity: polish.
- [5-E loss] **frustration**: Gravity Snare fired twice on Vex in two turns. After that he cost 8 Energy. Then Mind Blast hit for 7 (Void Resonance plus Amplify). I was at 9 Health with no way to see it coming. I didn't retry. Severity: minor (it's optional).
- [5-6 victory] **delight**: "HALCYON maps it gently, the last pattern it needs, and says nothing about why it wanted it." Chilling, and the perfect setup. But "She asks to speak to you while she still can" isn't followed by the conversation until the cutscene, which is fine.
- [s5 cutscene] **mixed**: The reveal is excellent but very compressed: the Choir is the Lamplighters, the Starfall was their first attempt, HALCYON was ANCHOR, and Directive 17 Clause 4. HALCYON leaving "to help from the inside" is one line from a recording. I wanted one more beat with HALCYON before it went. "Then the Choir took her, and Nyra died." is flat narration for a character I'd just started to care about. Severity: minor.
- [Singularity Pack] **delight + disappointment**: The flavour is excellent. "It still kneels the way it did when it was a person." "The voices the Choir forgot to keep." Mox on Black-Glass Sentinel. But the set is small. Two of the six packs I opened were 100% and 80% duplicates past the limit (scrap). One pack had two Glass Acolytes, another two Glass Lancers. Severity: minor.
- [Singularity cards, story] **observation**: It's slightly odd that the Fleet fields Choir Singers and Glass Acolytes (people the Choir absorbed). Nothing in the fiction explains why I can use them. Severity: polish.
- [ev-beacon + Echo of the Lost Fleet card] **lore contradiction**: Okonkwo says "Eleven hundred ships went down that night" (route event and card flavour). The Archive's official Inquiry summary says **Ships lost: 3,112**. The New Lagos testimony says "more than a thousand ships fell" on New Lagos alone. Severity: minor (Priya notices this kind of thing).
- [6-1 to 6-4 crates] **boredom**: Sector 6 supply crates offered Acid Rain, Supply Drop, Foresight, Chain Lightning and Lightning Arc, all old Academy cards. That felt like a let-down at the climax. Severity: minor.
- [6-2 victory] **polish**: Okonkwo's victory line repeats his briefing almost word for word ("The real Ixis loved her children…" then "She loved them, once. That thing never did.").
- [6-3] **too easy**: Calculus Omega never damaged me. Its armour gimmick barely mattered. Severity: minor.
- [6-E2, 6-E] **delight**: The Lost Fleet's victory ("The Meridian logs every hull number it can read") and Echo of HALCYON's ("Halcyon would have given you one. It would have been a pass.") were the two best victory texts of the session. **But both are optional Elites.** The HALCYON echo especially feels essential to HALCYON's arc. Okonkwo's 6-4 victory line ("The Choir made a glass copy of Halcyon too…") and HALCYON's finale line ("The Choir's glass copy of me was a puppet") both assume you played it. Severity: major (story structure).
- [6-E, attempt 1] **frustration**: I lost to fatigue after 23 turns. My deck was all removal and no reach. The defeat Advisor correctly told me to add draw cards or use Data Mine, which helped. Severity: minor.
- [6-5 phase changes] **delight**: "HALCYON reroutes power to you: +8 Health, +1 card", then "HALCYON gives you everything it has left: +8 Health, +2 cards." Story and mechanics agree perfectly. This is the moment the whole HALCYON arc was building to.
- [6-5 phase 3] **observation**: The Last Voice died in 2 turns (my Vex with Warp did the final 5 damage). The "spend everything now" phase was over almost before it started. A little anticlimactic mechanically, but the cutscene made up for it. Severity: polish.
- [Finale cutscene] **delight**: "Look after Anchorage. It was my home first." "Anchorage has its lights on." "Evaluation complete. Commander, you pass." It calls back to the s1 "You pass, Cadet" and lands emotionally. The Choir's "We were so many once, and so afraid of ending" gives the antagonist a soul.
- [Finale] **polish**: "The Lamps are safe" appears in both the victory text and the cutscene, back to back. HALCYON is spelled "HALCYON" in speaker labels and "Halcyon" in dialogue, which is inconsistent (deliberate?).
- [Proving Grounds] **observation**: The intro is purely mechanical ("draft 20 cards… 8 trials… hull points"), with no story frame. The Archive memo says HALCYON was built *for* the Proving Grounds, so a line about who runs it now (Okonkwo? an archived HALCYON sim?) would be an easy, poignant hook. Severity: polish.

## Quit points
None. The closest was the 5-E loss (I skipped that Elite) and the 23-turn fatigue loss in 6-E. The HALCYON story pulled me back for a second try.

## Debrief answers
1. **How to win:** reduce the enemy commander to 0 Health. Mechanics I learned: (a) Traps are hidden and fire on a condition, so you bait them with cheap cards or spells first; (b) Amplify adds damage to your spells, so kill those units first, and Nyra's +2 turns Hymn of Ending into a board wipe; (c) Guardian units must be attacked first, so they protect your commander and units behind them.
2. **Story:** A signal from the galactic core, the Choir, caused the Starfall seventy years ago and is now taking people who say yes: Vex, Ixis, Calculus Prime, Nyra. The Choir is the Lamplighters, the race that built the Lamps, who merged into one mind rather than die. HALCYON, the Academy AI, was secretly Anchorage's old station mind, kept alive by Directive 17 to train a commander who could reach the core. Main characters: HALCYON, Admiral Okonkwo, Nyra, Mox and the Commander (me).
3. **Best moment:** HALCYON rerouting power to me between the Choir's phases, then "Commander, you pass." **Worst:** fighting a frozen board in 5-2 for 17 turns. **Most confusing:** enemies playing cards not on their Deep-Scan list.
4. **Difficulty:** Sector 5 was steady but long and freeze-heavy. Sector 6 main missions were easier than Sector 5 (6-3 especially). The Elites were the real spikes (5-E, 6-E). The finale was well pitched.
5. **New cards:** The Singularity flavour is great and Nyra's Deploy (steal a unit) was the most fun card I played. The packs ran into duplicates fast, and the Sector 6 crates were dull.
6. **Cutscenes and story:** They are the reason I play. They add a great deal. s5 is crammed; the finale is earned.
7. **Play again tomorrow?** Yes, to reread the Archive and see what the remaining 6 records say. Proving Grounds alone wouldn't bring me back without a story hook.
8. **One sentence to a friend:** "It's a card game with a genuinely moving AI character, and the ending made me tear up a bit."

## Top 5 findings for the designers (most important first)
1. **HALCYON's arc depends on an optional Elite.** Echo of HALCYON (6-E) carries key emotional beats ("It would have been a pass"). Later main-path text (the 6-4 victory and the finale's "glass copy of me") assumes you played it. Players who skip it miss part of the payoff and meet references to events they never saw. Consider making it a main mission, or making the later lines stand on their own.
2. **The s5 reveal is overloaded and HALCYON's departure is rushed.** One cutscene delivers four big revelations, plus HALCYON leaving, in a recorded one-liner. Nyra's death is narrated in one flat line. The finale and the phase-change messages work brilliantly, so HALCYON deserves one more live beat before it goes.
3. **Lore contradiction on the Starfall's scale.** Okonkwo (ev-beacon and the Echo of the Lost Fleet card) says 1,100 ships were lost. The Archive's official Inquiry says 3,112. Story players who read the Archive will catch it.
4. **Sector 5 pacing: freeze-lock and long fights.** Cryo Nova, Glacial Titan and Cryo Sentry froze my entire board repeatedly. 5-2 took 17 turns and 5-4 took 16. Meanwhile 6-2 and 6-3 (Calculus Omega: zero damage taken) are noticeably easier than Sector 5. The difficulty curve dips right before the finale.
5. **Rewards and terminology friction at the climax.** Sector 6 crates offer old Academy commons. Singularity Packs hit duplicate-beyond-limit fast (one pack had 5 of 5 dupes, and packs contained same-card pairs). Card text says "Protocols" where everything else says "spells". Teaching is out of order: Traps are faced in Elite 5-E2 before 5-4's "NEW: Trap", and "NEW: Cryo" comes long after Frost Lance.

## Suggestions (opinion)
- Promote Echo of HALCYON to the main path (perhaps as 6-4.5), or keep the Elite and rewrite the dependent lines.
- Split s5 into Nyra's death and the HALCYON recording, and give HALCYON a live line before it leaves.
- Change Okonkwo's "Eleven hundred" to "Three thousand", or to "more than a thousand", which is still true.
- Cap consecutive full-board Cryo on Officer, or give Sector 5 decks one fewer Cryo Nova.
- Give Sector 6 crates Singularity cards, and stop packs from containing two copies of the same card.
- Retire, or re-voice, route events left over from earlier sectors once their villain is dead (Vex in "Drifting Freighter").
- Fix the Silent Foundry option so "finished unit" actually gives a unit.
- Give Proving Grounds a one-line story frame tied to HALCYON's original purpose.

## Bugs (with repro)
1. **Silent Foundry gives a spell for the "unit" option.** Repro: `event ev-foundry`, then `event ev-foundry 1` ("Take a finished unit off the line (+1 card)"). Result: "New card: Fortify" (a spell). Expected: a unit.
2. **Batched achievement payout.** Repro: after `new --from 5`, the first `event ev-freighter 3` printed 7 SERVICE RECORD awards at once (Commissioned, Debt Settled, Queen's Fall, …). Possibly expected for a generated save, but it reads like a backlog flush.
3. **Abandoning Proving Grounds before picking a power.** Repro: `challenge start`, then `challenge abandon`. It shows "hull 0/3 … RUN OVER: 0 trial(s) won. Collect with: challenge finish", which treats an unstarted run as lost. Minor.
4. **Log line ambiguity (possibly game text):**
   - When a Hive Larva transforms, the log says "enemy Brood Warrior was transformed." with no mention of the Larva (6-2).
   - Psionic Horror's random 2 damage appears under the enemy's spell with no source (6-4, after Frost Lance: "your Colonial Marine took 2 damage").
   - "took 2 damage (2 absorbed by Armor)" means 2 total, all absorbed, which is easy to misread as 4.

## Harness issues
- Every `attack`/`play` prints the full board state, so chaining commands produces very long output. Fine, but noisy.
- `attack Kade E1` (by name) isn't supported: "Usage: attack <attacker> <target>". Only slots and #tags work, while `play` accepts names. That's inconsistent.
- `power` with no target prints "Your power needs a target" and does nothing, even for a self-only heal (Nanite Repair). An obvious default (A0) would help.
- `card Warp` → "No such card". Keyword lookup isn't supported (the Codex covers some keywords).
- Grammar: "your The Recall Foreman", "your The Hivewarden".
- The `--from 5` save left 1,110 Alloy unspent and every station module at Mk 0, which is unlike a real player at that point.
